- The mean-square counterpart: the normalized eigenfunctions ([[§30 Expansion in Series of Eigenfunctions#^rem-30-1|Remark: Normalized Eigenfunctions and Parseval's Identity]]) form an orthonormal basis of the weighted $L^2$ space in the sense of [[§27 Orthonormal Sets and Bases#^def-27-4|556 Def. §27.4]], characterized by Parseval's equality in [[§27 Orthonormal Sets and Bases#^thm-27-8|556 Thm. §27.8]]; the case $\phi'' + \lambda^2\phi = 0$ with periodic conditions is the Fourier basis, [[§27 Orthonormal Sets and Bases#^thm-27-11|556 Thm. §27.11]].
- The uniform-convergence clause has the form of the Weierstrass M-test, [[§25 More on Uniform Convergence#^thm-25-3|451 Thm. §25.3]], for the series of normalized eigenfunctions, which are uniformly bounded for a regular problem.
