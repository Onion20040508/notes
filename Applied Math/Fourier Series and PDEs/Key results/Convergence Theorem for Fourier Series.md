## Connections
- In Functional Analysis the trigonometric functions form an orthonormal basis of $L^2$, [[§27 Orthonormal Sets and Bases#^thm-27-11|556 Thm. §27.11]], so every square-integrable $f$ is the sum of its Fourier series in the $L^2$ norm, "not necessarily pointwise" ([[§27 Orthonormal Sets and Bases#^rem-27-9|556 Remark: Fourier Series]]); Lay's version for continuous $f$ is [[§57 Applications of Inner Product Spaces#^thm-57-4|235 Thm. §57.4]]. [[§12 Convergence of Fourier Series#^thm-12-1|Theorem §12.1]] is the pointwise statement, at the price of the smoothness hypothesis; convergence in the mean is [[§15★ Mean Error and Convergence in Mean#^thm-15-6|Theorem §15.6]].
