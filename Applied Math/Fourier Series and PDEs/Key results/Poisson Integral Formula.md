## Connections
- The kernel $\frac{c^2 - r^2}{c^2 + r^2 - 2rc\cos(\theta - \phi)}$ is, up to the factor $\frac{1}{2\pi c}$, the normal derivative on the circle of the Green's function of the disk. The general representation of a harmonic function by its boundary values comes from Green's second identity, [[§28 The Divergence Theorem in Higher Dimensions and Green's Identities#^thm-28-3|452 Thm. §28.3]], applied with the fundamental solution $\ln r$, [[§29 Conservation of Mass and Laplace's Equation#^thm-29-2|452 Thm. §29.2]]; 452 develops these tools but does not compute the kernel of the disk. The half-plane counterpart is [[§47 Potential in Unbounded Regions#^rem-47-2|Remark: The Half-Plane and Its Poisson Formula]].
