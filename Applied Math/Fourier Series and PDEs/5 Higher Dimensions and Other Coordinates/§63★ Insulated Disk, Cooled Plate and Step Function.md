## The Insulated Disk

With an insulated edge, $R'(a) = 0$ replaces $R(a) = 0$: separation of variables is otherwise unchanged but admits a constant mode, and the radial eigenvalue problem takes its eigenvalues from the zeros of $J_1$.

## The Cooled Plate

A plate that loses heat through its faces gains a loss term like a rod with lateral convection: the thin rectangular plate has the steady state of a cooling fin, and the circular plate is solved with the modified Bessel function $I_0$.

## The Step Function on the Sphere

The step function, $-1$ on $-1 < x < 0$ and $1$ on $0 < x < 1$, has only odd Legendre coefficients. With $x = \cos\phi$ it describes a sphere whose hemispheres are held at opposite potentials, or at opposite temperatures.
