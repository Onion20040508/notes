*Chapter 2 examples revisited, unbounded rods: four initial temperatures that the chapter solves on the semi-infinite and the infinite rod and then evaluates in closed form.*

## The Heated Section

A block of initial temperature, $T_0$ on an interval and $0$ elsewhere, is the basic initial condition on an unbounded rod: at the end of a semi-infinite rod, in the center of an infinite rod, and on $0 < x < \pi$ (Homework 9, Problem 3). The error function gives all of them in closed form, which also evaluates the Fourier integral of the homework problem.

## The Half Sine Wave

The half sine wave, $\sin x$ on $0 < x < \pi$ and $0$ beyond, is the initial temperature of a semi-infinite rod with a fixed end and of an infinite rod (Practice Midterm 2, Questions 7 and 8), solved by a Fourier sine integral and by a full Fourier integral.

## The One-Sided Exponential

The function $e^{-x}$ for $x > 0$ and $0$ for $x < 0$, the first Fourier integral of Chapter 1, returns as the initial temperature of an infinite rod (Midterm 2, Question 2): it is solved by a Fourier integral, and completing the square writes the solution with the complementary error function.

## The Gaussian

A Gaussian initial temperature stays Gaussian, its variance growing by $2t$; its energy stays constant while its entropy increases.
