## The Function $1/z$

Its domain of definition is the punctured plane $z \ne 0$, and writing $1/z = \bar z/|z|^2$ separates its real and imaginary components.

![[§13 Functions and Mappings#^ex-13-1]]

From the definition of the derivative, $1/z$ is differentiable at every nonzero point, with derivative $-1/z^2$.

![[§19 Derivatives#^ex-19-1]]

The components found above satisfy the Cauchy–Riemann equations at every nonzero point, and the formula $f'(z) = u_x + iv_x$ gives the same derivative.

![[§21 Cauchy–Riemann Equations#^ex-21-3]]

So $1/z$ is analytic at each nonzero point, and the origin is a singular point.
