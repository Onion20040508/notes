*The recurring example of Chapter 2: the conjugate $\bar z$, the basic function that is continuous everywhere and differentiable nowhere. The items stay in their sections; this section gathers them in course order.*

## The Function $\bar z$

Its difference quotient is $\overline{\Delta z}/\Delta z$, which has different limits along the two axes, so $\bar z$ is nowhere differentiable.
