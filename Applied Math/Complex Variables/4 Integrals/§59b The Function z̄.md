*The recurring example of Chapter 4: the integral of the nowhere-analytic function $\bar z$ around closed contours, which is not zero. The items stay in their sections; this section gathers them in course order.*

## The Function $\bar z$

Along the right half of the circle $|z| = 2$, two parametrizations give the same value $4\pi i$.

![[§44 Contour Integrals#^ex-44-1]]

Around the unit circle $\bar z = 1/z$, and $\int_C \bar z\,dz = 2\pi i$ is the case $m = 0$, $n = 1$ of the integrals of $z^m\bar z^n$.

![[§44 Contour Integrals#^ex-44-2]]

Since $\bar z$ is not analytic, the Cauchy–Goursat theorem does not apply; Green's theorem shows instead that $\frac{1}{2i}\int_C \bar z\,dz$ is the area enclosed by $C$.

![[§51 Proof of the Theorem (Cauchy–Goursat Theorem)#^ex-51-2]]

The same nonzero integral shows that the continuous function $\bar z$ fails the hypothesis of Morera's theorem (part (a)).
