*The recurring example of Chapter 5: the geometric series, the model for every power series and the source of most Taylor and Laurent series in the chapter. The items stay in their sections; this section gathers them in course order.*

## The Geometric Series

The series $\sum z^n$ converges to $1/(1 - z)$ when $|z| < 1$ and diverges when $|z| \ge 1$.

![[§61 Convergence of Series#^ex-61-1]]

Its real and imaginary parts at $z = re^{i\theta}$ give two real series.

![[§61 Convergence of Series#^ex-61-2]]

Substitution and multiplication by powers of $z$ turn it into the series of related rational functions.

![[§64 Examples (Proof of Taylor's Theorem)#^ex-64-1]]

The convergence is uniform on each closed disk $|z| \le R_1$ with $R_1 < 1$, but not on the whole open disk.
