## Connections
- [[§27★ Harmonic Functions#^thm-27-1|Theorem §27.1]] is the source of the closed-form solutions of potential problems; the converse direction (every harmonic function on a simply connected domain is the real part of an analytic function, its **harmonic conjugate** being the imaginary part) is [[§115★ Harmonic Conjugates#^thm-115-4|Theorem §115.4]].
