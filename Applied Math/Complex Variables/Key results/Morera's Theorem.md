## Connections
- On a simply connected domain Morera's theorem is, for continuous functions, the converse of the Cauchy–Goursat theorem in the form [[§52 Simply Connected Domains#^thm-52-1|Theorem §52.1]]. On a multiply connected domain its hypothesis is sufficient but not necessary: $1/z$ is analytic for $z \ne 0$ but $\int_{|z|=1}dz/z = 2\pi i$, [[§57 Some Consequences of the Extension#^ex-57-2|Example §57.2]].
