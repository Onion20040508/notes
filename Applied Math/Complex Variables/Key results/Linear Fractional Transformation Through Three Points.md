## Connections
- The transformation is unique, [[§100★ An Implicit Form#^thm-100-3|Theorem §100.3]]: if $S$ and $T$ both send $z_1, z_2, z_3$ to $w_1, w_2, w_3$, then $S^{-1} \circ T$ is linear fractional ([[§99★ Linear Fractional Transformations#^prop-99-5|Proposition §99.5]]) with three fixed points, hence the identity. Composition corresponds to multiplying coefficient matrices, so these maps form a group, the image of $GL_2(\mathbb{C})$, [[§3 Basic Examples of Groups#^def-3-6|493 Def. §3.6]].
