## Connections
- For a circle $C$ centered at $z_0$, formula (1) of [[§54 Cauchy Integral Formula#^thm-54-1|Theorem §54.1]] says that $f(z_0)$ is the average of $f$ over the circle: Gauss's mean value theorem, [[§59 Maximum Modulus Principle#^thm-59-1|Theorem §59.1]]. Its real part is the mean value property of harmonic functions, [[§39 Potential in a Disk#^thm-39-4|341 Thm. §39.4]], and the Poisson integral formula [[§39 Potential in a Disk#^thm-39-3|341 Thm. §39.3]] is the corresponding statement that a harmonic function in a disk is determined by its boundary values.
