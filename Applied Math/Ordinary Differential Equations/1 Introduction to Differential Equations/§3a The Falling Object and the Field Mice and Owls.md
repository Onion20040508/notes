*The two recurring models of Chapter 1, an object falling against air resistance and a population of field mice preyed on by owls, gathered in course order. The items themselves stay in their sections.*

Both models have the form $dy/dt = ay - b$. They are formulated in [[§1 Some Basic Mathematical Models; Direction Fields|§1]] and studied there through their direction fields, solved in [[§2 Solutions of Some Differential Equations|§2]], and compared by the solution formula of [[§2 Solutions of Some Differential Equations#^thm-2-1|Theorem §2.1]].
