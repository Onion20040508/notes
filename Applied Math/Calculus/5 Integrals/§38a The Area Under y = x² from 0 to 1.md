*Chapter 5 example revisited: the area under the parabola $y = x^2$ from $0$ to $1$, followed through the chapter from estimate to one-line evaluation.*

## The Area Under y = x² from 0 to 1

The area under the parabola $y = x^2$ from $0$ to $1$ is first estimated with rectangles, then computed exactly as the limit $\frac13$ of the right-endpoint sums. As the integral $\int_0^1 x^2\,dx = \frac13$ it is an input to the properties of the definite integral, and the Fundamental Theorem of Calculus finally evaluates it in one line.
