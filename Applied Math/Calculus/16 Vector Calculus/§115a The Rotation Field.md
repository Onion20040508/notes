## The Rotation Field

The field $\mathbf{F}(x, y) = -y\,\mathbf{i} + x\,\mathbf{j}$ is the velocity field of a wheel rotating counterclockwise. It is drawn in [[§107 Vector Fields|§107]]:

![[§107 Vector Fields#^ex-107-1]]

It is not conservative ([[§107 Vector Fields|§107]]). In space, as $\langle -y, x, 0 \rangle$, its curl is $\langle 0, 0, 2 \rangle \ne \mathbf{0}$, which confirms this:

![[§111 Curl and Divergence#^ex-111-3]]

Dividing it by $x^2 + y^2$ gives the field with a singularity at the origin of [[§110a Extended Versions of Green's Theorem#^ex-110-5|Example §110.5]].
