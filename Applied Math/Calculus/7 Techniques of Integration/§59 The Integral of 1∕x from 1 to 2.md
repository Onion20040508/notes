*Chapter 7 example revisited: the integral $\int_1^2 \frac1x\,dx = \ln 2 = 0.693147\ldots$, the test case for the approximation rules.*

## The Integral of 1∕x from 1 to 2

The exact value $\int_1^2 \frac{dx}{x} = \ln 2$ is known, so the integral measures how good each rule is. With $n = 5$ the Trapezoidal and Midpoint Rules are compared with $\ln 2$, the error bounds tell how large $n$ must be for accuracy within $0.0001$, and Simpson's Rule reaches that accuracy with $n = 8$, against $n = 41$ for the Trapezoidal Rule and $n = 29$ for the Midpoint Rule.
