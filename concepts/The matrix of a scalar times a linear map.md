> [!theorem] 3.38 The matrix of a scalar times a linear map
> If $\lambda\in\F$ and $T\in\Lin(V,W)$, then $\mathcal{M}(\lambda T)=\lambda\mathcal{M}(T)$.

> [!proof]-
> *(Filled in.)* $(\lambda T)v_k=\lambda\sum_jA_{j,k}w_j=\sum_j(\lambda A_{j,k})w_j$.
