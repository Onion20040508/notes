> [!definition] 3.97 Translate
> For $v\in V$ and a subset $U\subseteq V$, the set $v+U=\{v+u : u\in U\}$ is a *translate* of $U$.

> [!remark] Picture
> For $U$ a line through $0$ in $\R^2$, the translates of $U$ are the lines parallel to $U$.

## Connections
- Translates of a subspace partition $V$: [[Two translates of a subspace are equal or disjoint]]. They are the points of [[Quotient space, V∕U]]. Solution sets of solvable inhomogeneous systems are translates of the null space.
