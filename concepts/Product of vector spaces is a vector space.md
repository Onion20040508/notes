> [!theorem] 3.89 Product of vector spaces is a vector space
> If $V_1,\dots,V_m$ are vector spaces over $\F$, then $V_1\times\dots\times V_m$ is a vector space over $\F$.

> [!proof]-
> *(Filled in; left to the reader in Axler.)* Every axiom of [[Vector space]] is checked slot by slot, where it holds because it holds in each $V_k$. The additive identity is $(0,\dots,0)$ (the $k$-th $0$ in $V_k$) and the inverse of $(v_1,\dots,v_m)$ is $(-v_1,\dots,-v_m)$.
