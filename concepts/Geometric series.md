- $\sum a^n=\tfrac1{1-a}$ iff $|a|<1$ ([[MATH 451 §14 Series#^ex-14-4|§14]])
- Pointwise but not uniform convergence on $(-1,1)$ ([[MATH 451 §24 Uniform Convergence#^ex-24-1|§24]])
- Term-by-term differentiation: $\sum nx^n=\tfrac{x}{(1-x)^2}$ ([[MATH 451 §26 Differentiation and Integration of Power Series#^ex-26-1|§26]])
- Term-by-term integration: $\sum \tfrac{x^n}{n}=-\ln(1-x)$ ([[MATH 451 §26 Differentiation and Integration of Power Series#^ex-26-2|§26]])
