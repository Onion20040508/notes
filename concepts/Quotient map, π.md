> [!definition] 3.104 Quotient map, π
> For a subspace $U$ of $V$, the *quotient map* $\pi:V\to V/U$ is $\pi(v)=v+U$.

> [!remark] Linear
> *(Filled in.)* $\pi(v+w)=(v+w)+U=(v+U)+(w+U)=\pi(v)+\pi(w)$ and $\pi(\lambda v)=\lambda\pi(v)$, directly from [[Addition and scalar multiplication on V∕U]].

## Connections
- $\nullsp\pi=U$ and $\range\pi=V/U$: used in [[Dimension of quotient space]]. Factors every map through it: [[Null space and range of ̃T]].
