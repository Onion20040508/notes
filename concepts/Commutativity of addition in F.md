> [!theorem] 1.14 Commutativity of addition in F
> If $x,y\in\F^n$, then $x+y=y+x$.

> [!proof]-
> Write $x=(x_1,\dots,x_n)$, $y=(y_1,\dots,y_n)$. By [[Addition in Fⁿ]] and commutativity in $\F$,
> $$
> x+y=(x_1+y_1,\dots,x_n+y_n)=(y_1+x_1,\dots,y_n+x_n)=y+x.
> $$
