> [!definition] 1.13 Addition in Fⁿ
> Addition in $\F^n$ is coordinatewise:
> $$
> (x_1,\dots,x_n)+(y_1,\dots,y_n)=(x_1+y_1,\dots,x_n+y_n).
> $$
