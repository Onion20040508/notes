> [!theorem] 3.60 Inverse is unique
> An invertible linear map has a unique inverse.

> [!proof]-
> If $S_1,S_2$ are inverses of $T$, then $S_1=S_1I=S_1(TS_2)=(S_1T)S_2=IS_2=S_2$.
