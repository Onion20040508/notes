> [!definition] 1.18 Scalar multiplication in Fⁿ
> For $\lambda\in\F$ and $(x_1,\dots,x_n)\in\F^n$,
> $$
> \lambda(x_1,\dots,x_n)=(\lambda x_1,\dots,\lambda x_n).
> $$

> [!remark] Scalar times vector
> The output is a vector. Contrast the [[Dot product]], which takes two vectors and returns a scalar; that idea is generalized by the [[Inner product]] in Chapter 6. In $\R^2$, $\lambda x$ stretches or shrinks $x$ by $|\lambda|$ and reverses it when $\lambda<0$.

## Connections
- Together with [[Addition in Fⁿ]] this makes $\F^n$ a vector space ([[Vector space]]).
