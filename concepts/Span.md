> [!definition] 2.4 Span
> The *span* of $v_1,\dots,v_m$ is the set of all their linear combinations:
> $$
> \Span(v_1,\dots,v_m)=\{a_1v_1+\dots+a_mv_m : a_1,\dots,a_m\in\F\}.
> $$
> The span of the empty list is $\{0\}$.
