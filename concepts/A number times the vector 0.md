> [!theorem] 1.31 A number times the vector 0
> $a0=0$ for every $a\in\F$ (vector $0$ on both sides).

> [!proof]-
> $a0=a(0+0)=a0+a0$. Add the additive inverse of $a0$ to both sides to get $0=a0$.
