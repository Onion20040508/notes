**What the exam rewards.** The aim is to understand QFT through its definitions, derivations and calculation techniques, not to memorize. Three layers per topic:
- **Language**: definitions and conventions stated exactly, with every sign and factor of $2$, $\pi$, $i$, and which source writes it how (course vs Peskin–Schroeder (PS) vs Yu: [[Larsen PHY 513]]);
- **Derivations**: ★★ the core derivations, done on the slides; ★ the supporting ones. Each names its technique (K1–K16, listed at the end);
- **Drill**: every submitted problem-set part, broken into small questions in [[PHY 513 PS Drill]]; definitions, core derivations and everything Larsen left as an exercise, broken the same way in [[PHY 513 Notes Drill]].

**How to use this note.**
- Tick a box only when you can do it on paper, closed book, with signs and factors right.
- ★★ marks a core derivation (done on the slides), ★ a supporting one; these are review levels. A ★ in a note's name (§C3.6★) or before a box's kind word ("★ Theorem") is the notes' own mark for material beyond the course.
