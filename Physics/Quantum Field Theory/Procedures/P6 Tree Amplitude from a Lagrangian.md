# P6 Tree Amplitude from a Lagrangian

Turns an interaction Lagrangian and a process into the lowest-order amplitude $i\mathcal M$: which processes the interaction allows, which diagrams contribute, and the value of each. Inputs: a Lagrangian of scalar fields, and of Dirac fields coupled through fermion bilinears (Yukawa theory, QED), with an interaction without derivatives, and the incoming and outgoing particles with their momenta. Derivations are in the sections linked at each step; conventions in [[Larsen PHY 513]]; the output feeds [[P7 From ℳ to σ or Γ]]; for an unpolarized process with spin the whole route is [[P8 Unpolarized Cross Section from Diagrams]].

## Steps

1. **Read the Lagrangian.** Separate $\mathcal L = \mathcal L_0 + \mathcal L_{\rm int}$; list the fields, their masses and kinds (a real field; $\phi$ and $\phi^\dagger$ of a complex field), and write $\mathcal L_{\rm int} = -\sum_tg_tM_t$ with monomials $M_t = \prod_k\phi_k^{m_{t,k}}$ ([[§C6a.14 Feynman Rules for Polynomial Interactions#^def-c6a-14-1|Def. §C6a.14.1]]). Name the setup: a Model box of the theory ([[§C6a.1 From Free to Interacting Fields#^mod-c6a-1-1|Model §C6a.1.1]], [[§C6a.9 Position-Space Feynman Rules#^mod-c6a-9-2|Model §C6a.9.2]], [[§C6a.9 Position-Space Feynman Rules#^mod-c6a-9-3|Model §C6a.9.3]], [[§C6b.6 Decay Kinematics#^mod-c6b-6-4|Model §C6b.6.4]]), an instance of [[§C6a.0 Setups and Observables#^mod-c6a-0-3|Model §C6a.0.3]]. *With fermions:* the Dirac field enters as $\bar\psi$ and $\psi$, two kinds of legs, fermion number conserved ([[§C6d.1 Yukawa Theory#^mod-c6d-1-1|Model §C6d.1.1]], [[§C6d.1 Yukawa Theory#^thm-c6d-1-5|Theorem §C6d.1.5]]). ^p6-1

2. **Vertex factors.** Each monomial is a vertex with $r_t = \sum_km_{t,k}$ legs and factor $-ig_t\prod_km_{t,k}!$ ([[§C6a.14 Feynman Rules for Polynomial Interactions#^thm-c6a-14-2|Theorem §C6a.14.2]]): $-\frac{g}{3!}\phi^3 \to -ig$, $-\frac{\lambda}{4!}\phi^4 \to -i\lambda$, $-\mu\Phi\phi^2 \to -2i\mu$, $-\frac\lambda4(\phi^\dagger\phi)^2 \to -i\lambda$; $-g\bar\psi\psi\phi \to -ig$ (three distinct legs, no factorial: [[§C6d.4 First Order#^thm-c6d-4-4|Theorem §C6d.4.4]]); $-e\bar\psi\gamma^\mu\psi A_\mu \to -ie\gamma^\mu$, a matrix with a Lorentz index ([[§C8.4 Feynman Rules for QED#^thm-c8-4-3|Theorem §C8.4.3]]). ^p6-2

3. **Enumerate the diagrams.** *First order:* list every split of the legs of $M_t$ into incoming particles, outgoing particles and pairs closed into loops, $\langle f|i\hat T^{(1)}|i\rangle = -ig\prod_k\frac{m_k!}{2^{\ell_k}\ell_k!}D_F^{(m_k)}(0)^{\ell_k}(2\pi)^4\delta^4(\cdots)$ ([[§C6c.4 First Order꞉ Enumerate, then Filter#^thm-c6c-4-1|Theorem §C6c.4.1]]), then keep what energy-momentum conservation allows on shell ([[§C6c.4 First Order꞉ Enumerate, then Filter#^thm-c6c-4-2|Theorem §C6c.4.2]]). *Given order* $\prod_tg_t^{n_t}$: count legs $r = \sum_tn_tr_t$, external particles $n_{\rm ext}$, internal lines $I = \frac{r - n_{\rm ext}}{2}$ (none if $r - n_{\rm ext}$ is odd), loops $L = I - \sum_tn_t + 1$ ([[§C6a.14 Feynman Rules for Polynomial Interactions#^thm-c6a-14-3|Theorem §C6a.14.3]], 1–2); draw every fully connected diagram with $L = 0$ whose external points are the labelled particles ([[§C6c.6 Feynman Rules for Amplitudes#^def-c6c-6-1|Def. §C6c.6.1]]): distribute the particles over the vertices and discard the distributions that leave legs of one vertex to pair among themselves (a vertex with all its legs on particles is a piece of its own: [[§C6a.14 Feynman Rules for Polynomial Interactions#^thm-c6a-14-3|Theorem §C6a.14.3]], 3). A tree is fixed by its shape and by which particles sit at which vertex ([[§C6c.6 Feynman Rules for Amplitudes#^thm-c6c-6-4|Theorem §C6c.6.4]], 1); terms with a particle passing through, bubbles and loops on legs are not part of $i\hat T$ ([[§C6c.4 First Order꞉ Enumerate, then Filter#^thm-c6c-4-6|Theorem §C6c.4.6]], [[§C6c.5 Second Order꞉ Loops, Amputation and Symmetry Factors#^thm-c6c-5-3|Theorem §C6c.5.3]]). ^p6-3

4. **Apply the tree rules to each diagram.** External line $1$; vertex $-ig_t\prod_km_{t,k}!$; internal line $\frac{i}{q^2 - m_j^2 + i\varepsilon}$ with $q$ fixed by conservation at the vertices; $S = 1$ ([[§C6c.6 Feynman Rules for Amplitudes#^thm-c6c-6-2|Theorem §C6c.6.2]], [[§C6c.5 Second Order꞉ Loops, Amputation and Symmetry Factors#^thm-c6c-5-5|Theorem §C6c.5.5]], [[§C6c.5 Second Order꞉ Loops, Amputation and Symmetry Factors#^thm-c6c-5-6|Theorem §C6c.5.6]]). Loops, at higher orders: the same rules with $\int\frac{d^4q}{(2\pi)^4}$ per loop and $\frac1S$, external legs amputated ([[§C6c.6 Feynman Rules for Amplitudes#^thm-c6c-6-3|Theorem §C6c.6.3]], [[§C6c.5 Second Order꞉ Loops, Amputation and Symmetry Factors#^def-c6c-5-1|Def. §C6c.5.1]]). *With fermions* ([[§C6d.6 Momentum-Space Feynman Rules#^thm-c6d-6-2|Theorem §C6d.6.2]]): external $u$ (incoming fermion), $\bar u$ (outgoing fermion), $\bar v$ (incoming antifermion), $v$ (outgoing antifermion) ([[§C6d.2 Contractions with External Fermions#^thm-c6d-2-1|Theorem §C6d.2.1]]); internal $\frac{i(\slashed q + m)}{q^2 - m^2 + i\varepsilon}$ with $q$ along the arrow; each fermion line written against its arrow, a number ([[§C6d.2 Contractions with External Fermions#^thm-c6d-2-4|Theorem §C6d.2.4]]); a closed fermion loop $-1$ and a trace ([[§C6d.4 First Order#^thm-c6d-4-6|Theorem §C6d.4.6]]); $S = 1$ ([[§C6d.4 First Order#^thm-c6d-4-7|Theorem §C6d.4.7]]). *With photons* ([[§C8.4 Feynman Rules for QED#^thm-c8-4-4|Theorem §C8.4.4]]): internal photon $\frac{-ig_{\mu\nu}}{q^2 + i\varepsilon}$, external photon $\epsilon_\mu(p)$ (incoming), $\epsilon^\ast_\mu(p)$ (outgoing), physical polarizations only; every index $\mu$ of a vertex $-ie\gamma^\mu$ contracted with a photon factor. ^p6-4

5. **Sum the channels.** Add all diagrams of the order: for $2 \to 2$ with one internal line, the $s$-, $t$- and $u$-channels, $q = p_A + p_B$, $p_A - p_1$, $p_A - p_2$ ([[§C6c.6 Feynman Rules for Amplitudes#^ex-c6c-6-1|Example §C6c.6.1]]). For scalar fields every diagram enters with the same sign; relative signs between diagrams come with fermions (the ordering rule of Yu §7.1, p. 268; [[§C6d.3 Wick's Theorem with Fermions and the Contraction Rule#^thm-c6d-3-4|Theorem §C6d.3.4]], [[§C6d.5 Second Order#^thm-c6d-5-3|Theorem §C6d.5.3]]). *With fermions:* fix the order of the external states ([[§C6d.2 Contractions with External Fermions#^def-c6d-2-2|Def. §C6d.2.2]]), give each diagram the sign $(-1)^{\#\text{crossings}}$ of its contractions ([[§C6d.3 Wick's Theorem with Fermions and the Contraction Rule#^thm-c6d-3-4|Theorem §C6d.3.4]]); diagrams that differ by exchanging identical external fermions have opposite signs ([[§C6d.5 Second Order#^thm-c6d-5-3|Theorem §C6d.5.3]]; in general [[§C6d.5 Second Order#^thm-c6d-5-4|Theorem §C6d.5.4]]: exchanged fermion partners $-$, exchanged bosons $+$); the overall sign follows the order of the external states and, with each bra the Hermitian conjugate of its ket, is physical relative to the unscattered term ([[§C6d.5 Second Order#^rem-c6d-5-1|§C6d.5, Remark: The overall sign]], [[§C8.5 The Coulomb Potential, and Why Like Charges Repel#^thm-c8-5-6|Theorem §C8.5.6]]); the diagram alone does not show the relative sign ([[§C6d.6 Momentum-Space Feynman Rules#^cau-c6d-6-1|§C6d.6, Caution: Relative signs still need the contractions]]). For identical final particles the exchange $p_1 \leftrightarrow p_2$ is already in the list (it maps $t$ to $u$); it is not added again. For the cubic–quartic theory: $i\mathcal M = -ig^2\bigl[\frac{1}{s - m^2} + \frac{1}{t - m^2} + \frac{1}{u - m^2}\bigr] - i\lambda$ ([[§C6c.6 Feynman Rules for Amplitudes#^thm-c6c-6-4|Theorem §C6c.6.4]]). ^p6-5

6. **Read off $i\mathcal M$.** The sum multiplies $(2\pi)^4\delta^4(\sum p_{\rm in} - \sum p_{\rm out})$; strip it ([[§C6b.1 The S-Matrix and the Invariant Amplitude#^def-c6b-1-3|Def. §C6b.1.3]]) and check the dimension, $[\mathcal M] = 4 - N$ for $N$ external particles ([[§C6b.1 The S-Matrix and the Invariant Amplitude#^thm-c6b-1-5|Theorem §C6b.1.5]]). ^p6-6

7. **State the scope.** The result is the lowest order: free relativistic states over $|0\rangle$ stand for the in and out states ([[§C6b.1 The S-Matrix and the Invariant Amplitude#^pr-c6b-1-3|Principle §C6b.1.3]], [[§C6b.1 The S-Matrix and the Invariant Amplitude#^thm-c6b-1-1|Theorem §C6b.1.1]]); beyond it external lines carry $\sqrt Z$ and the physical mass ([[§C6c.1 Amplitudes from Correlation Functions#^cau-c6c-1-2|§C6c.1, Caution: The rule is stated at lowest order]]); an unstable initial particle is outside the asymptotic setup ([[§C6b.5 Decay Widths#^cau-c6b-5-1|§C6b.5, Caution: Free states in a decay amplitude]]). ^p6-7

## Pitfalls

- **Kinematics before dynamics.** A matrix element that $\mathcal L_{\rm int}$ allows may have no on-shell support: every $1 \to n$ with equal masses, every $0 \to n$ ([[§C6c.4 First Order꞉ Enumerate, then Filter#^thm-c6c-4-2|Theorem §C6c.4.2]]).
- **The factorials.** The vertex factor is $-ig_t\prod_km_{t,k}!$ whatever the normalization of $\mathcal L_{\rm int}$; a coupling written without $\frac{1}{m!}$ ($\mu\Phi\phi^2$) keeps the $m!$ in the vertex ($-2i\mu$).
- **A line straight through is a $\delta$, not a $D_F$**: it is the $\mathbb 1$, not part of $i\mathcal M$ ([[§C6c.3 Wick's Theorem between Particle States#^def-c6c-3-1|Def. §C6c.3.1]], [[§C6c.3 Wick's Theorem between Particle States#^cau-c6c-3-1|§C6c.3, Caution: A line is a δ, not a D_F]]).
- **Fully connected only.** Pieces in which a particle passes by, vacuum bubbles and loops on external legs are not part of $\mathcal M$ ([[§C6c.1 Amplitudes from Correlation Functions#^thm-c6c-1-2|Theorem §C6c.1.2]], [[§C6c.1 Amplitudes from Correlation Functions#^cau-c6c-1-1|§C6c.1, Caution: "Connected" here means fully connected]]).
- **No $\frac{1}{S_{\rm id}}$ in $\mathcal M$.** The identical-particle factor belongs to the integrated rate ([[P7 From ℳ to σ or Γ#^p7-7|P7, step 7]]); tree diagrams have $S = 1$.
- **External momenta are labels.** Exchanging two external particles gives another diagram, not a symmetry ([[§C6c.6 Feynman Rules for Amplitudes#^def-c6c-6-1|Def. §C6c.6.1]], 1).
