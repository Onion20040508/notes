# P9 Helicity Amplitudes

Turns a process with spin-$\frac12$ particles at high energy into its squared amplitudes and cross sections for definite helicities, spin by spin instead of averaged: either by projecting each spinor and squaring with traces that now contain $\gamma^5$, or by inserting explicit helicity spinors and computing the amplitude itself. It is the strategy of Lecture 20 (slide 6: "Helicity amplitudes: project each spinor on to helicity component; evaluate as before"; PS §5.2) and the user's Principle "Helicity amplitudes: procedure" (Lecture 20 update, §20.5). Steps 1, 3–6 follow [[P8 Unpolarized Cross Section from Diagrams]] with the changes stated; step 7 is the second method. Derivations are in the sections linked at each step; conventions in [[Larsen PHY 513]].

## Steps

1. **Write the diagram and $i\mathcal M$** with spinors $u$, $\bar u$, $v$, $\bar v$ ([[P8 Unpolarized Cross Section from Diagrams#^p8-1|P8, step 1]], [[P8 Unpolarized Cross Section from Diagrams#^p8-2|P8, step 2]]; for $e^+e^- \to \mu^+\mu^-$: [[§C8.6 Electron–Positron Annihilation꞉ the Amplitude and the Virtual Photon#^thm-c8-6-2|Theorem §C8.6.2]]). Set the fermion masses to zero, or work at $E \gg m$; state it ([[§C8.15 Chirality, Helicity and the QED Vertex#^rem-c8-15-3|§C8.15, Remark: Frame and mass]]). ^p9-1

2. **Project each spinor onto a helicity** with $P_{L,R} = \frac12(1 \mp \gamma^5)$ ([[§C8.15 Chirality, Helicity and the QED Vertex#^def-c8-15-1|Def. §C8.15.1]]): for a particle $P_Ru$ is right-handed ([[§C8.15 Chirality, Helicity and the QED Vertex#^thm-c8-15-1|Theorem §C8.15.1]]); for an antiparticle the reading is reversed, $P_Rv$ is left-handed ([[§C8.15 Chirality, Helicity and the QED Vertex#^thm-c8-15-2|Theorem §C8.15.2]]). At a QED vertex one projector projects both fermions ([[§C8.15 Chirality, Helicity and the QED Vertex#^thm-c8-15-4|Theorem §C8.15.4]]), so one projector per vertex fixes a pair of opposite physical helicities, and pairs of equal physical helicity give zero ([[§C8.15 Chirality, Helicity and the QED Vertex#^thm-c8-15-5|Theorem §C8.15.5]]). Read off which physical helicities the projected amplitude describes ([[§C8.16 Polarized Cross Sections for Muon Pair Production#^thm-c8-16-1|Theorem §C8.16.1]]). ^p9-2

3. **Square and sum over spins anyway.** Conjugate each bilinear with the projector kept to the right of the $\gamma$ ([[§C8.16 Polarized Cross Sections for Muon Pair Production#^thm-c8-16-2|Theorem §C8.16.2]]), sum over all spin labels with the completeness relations, and move the projectors together ($P^2 = P$): each line is a trace with one $\frac12(1 \pm \gamma^5)$ ([[§C8.16 Polarized Cross Sections for Muon Pair Production#^thm-c8-16-3|Theorem §C8.16.3]]). The projectors select the helicities: no $\frac14$. ^p9-3

4. **Evaluate the traces with $\gamma^5$** ([[§C5a.13 Gamma-Matrix Technology#^thm-c5a-13-3|Theorem §C5a.13.3]], [[§C5a.13 Gamma-Matrix Technology#^thm-c5a-13-4|Theorem §C5a.13.4]]): each line gives half the unpolarized tensor plus an antisymmetric $\mp2i\varepsilon$ term ([[§C8.16 Polarized Cross Sections for Muon Pair Production#^thm-c8-16-4|Theorem §C8.16.4]]). Contract: symmetric × antisymmetric $= 0$; $\varepsilon\varepsilon$ with the Minkowski identity, coefficient $-2$ ([[§C1a.5 Vectors, Tensors and Index Notation#^thm-c1a-5-4|Theorem §C1a.5.4]]). The $\varepsilon\varepsilon$ terms combine with the others, and the result is usually simpler than the unpolarized one ([[§C8.16 Polarized Cross Sections for Muon Pair Production#^thm-c8-16-5|Theorem §C8.16.5]]). ^p9-4

5. **Choose a frame and compute the polarized cross sections** ([[P8 Unpolarized Cross Section from Diagrams#^p8-5|P8, step 5]], [[P8 Unpolarized Cross Section from Diagrams#^p8-6|P8, step 6]] with $|\mathcal M|^2$ of one configuration in place of the average; [[§C8.16 Polarized Cross Sections for Muon Pair Production#^thm-c8-16-6|Theorem §C8.16.6]]). Use parity for the configurations with all helicities reversed, and check the zeros against angular momentum along the axis of motion ([[§C8.16 Polarized Cross Sections for Muon Pair Production#^thm-c8-16-8|Theorem §C8.16.8]]). ^p9-5

6. **Check against the unpolarized result**: average over the initial and sum over the final helicities; the result must be $\frac14\sum_{\rm spins}|\mathcal M|^2$ of P8 at the same masses ([[§C8.16 Polarized Cross Sections for Muon Pair Production#^thm-c8-16-7|Theorem §C8.16.7]]). ^p9-6

7. **Or insert explicit helicity spinors** in a chosen frame, $u = \sqrt{2E}(\frac12(1 - \hat{\mathbf p}\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma)\xi, \frac12(1 + \hat{\mathbf p}\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma)\xi)$ with $\hat{\mathbf p}\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma\,\xi = \pm\xi$, $v$ with a minus sign on the lower half ([[§C8.16 Polarized Cross Sections for Muon Pair Production#^thm-c8-16-9|Theorem §C8.16.9]]), and compute the currents with $\gamma^0\gamma^\mu = \operatorname{diag}(\bar\sigma^\mu, \sigma^\mu)$ ([[§C8.15 Chirality, Helicity and the QED Vertex#^thm-c8-15-3|Theorem §C8.15.3]]): each current is a polarization-like four-vector, outgoing ones conjugated, those along other axes rotated, and $\mathcal M$ is their Minkowski product ([[§C8.16 Polarized Cross Sections for Muon Pair Production#^thm-c8-16-10|Theorem §C8.16.10]]). Overall phases depend on the conventions for $\xi$, $\eta$; compare $|\mathcal M|^2$ with step 5. ^p9-7

## Pitfalls

- **Antiparticles reverse the reading**: $P_Rv$ is a left-handed antiparticle; the two-spinor of $v$ shows the opposite of the physical spin ([[§C8.15 Chirality, Helicity and the QED Vertex#^thm-c8-15-2|Theorem §C8.15.2]]).
- **The projector of the conjugate stays to the right of the $\gamma$**; $P_R$ and $P_L$ in one trace give zero ([[§C8.16 Polarized Cross Sections for Muon Pair Production#^cau-c8-16-1|§C8.16, Caution: The projector of the conjugate stands to the right of γ]]).
- **No $\frac14$** for one helicity configuration ([[§C8.16 Polarized Cross Sections for Muon Pair Production#^cau-c8-16-2|§C8.16, Caution: No ¼ for one helicity configuration]]).
- **$\varepsilon\varepsilon$ has $-2$ in Minkowski space**, and the order of the momenta in each $\varepsilon$ term follows the fermion line ([[§C8.16 Polarized Cross Sections for Muon Pair Production#^cau-c8-16-3|§C8.16, Caution: The εε contraction has −2 in Minkowski space]]).
- **The sign of the $\varepsilon$ trace depends on the convention** for $\varepsilon^{0123}$ ([[§C5a.13 Gamma-Matrix Technology#^cau-c5a-13-1|§C5a.13, Caution: The sign of the ε trace across sources]]).
- **Helicity of a massive particle is frame dependent**; the projectors select it only for $m = 0$ ([[§C8.15 Chirality, Helicity and the QED Vertex#^rem-c8-15-3|§C8.15, Remark: Frame and mass]]).
