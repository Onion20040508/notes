# P5 Symmetry Factor of a Diagram

Turns a Feynman diagram of a polynomial interaction into its coefficient: the symmetry factor $S$, so that the diagram enters with $\frac1S\prod(\text{vertex factors})\int\prod(\text{lines})$. Two routes that must agree: count the complete contractions that produce the diagram, or find its automorphisms. Derivations are in the sections linked at each step; the group theory in [[§CA.8 Pairings, Diagrams and Symmetry Factors|§CA.8]] and [[§CA.9 Splitting Pairings and the Exponential Formula|§CA.9]]; conventions in [[Larsen PHY 513]].

## Steps

1. **Write the term of the Dyson series that contains the diagram.** For $\phi^4$ with $n$ vertices and external fields at $x_1, \dots, x_{n_{\rm ext}}$: $\frac{1}{n!}\bigl(\frac{-i\lambda}{4!}\bigr)^n\int d^4z_1\cdots d^4z_n\,\langle0|T\{\hat\phi_I(x_1)\cdots\hat\phi_I(x_{n_{\rm ext}})\,\hat\phi_I(z_1)^4\cdots\hat\phi_I(z_n)^4\}|0\rangle$ ([[§C6a.8 Position-Space Feynman Rules#^thm-c6a-8-1|Theorem §C6a.8.1]]). For general vertices $\mathcal L_{\rm int} = -\sum_tg_tM_t$, $M_t = \prod_k\phi_k^{m_{t,k}}$, with $n_t$ vertices of type $t$: the prefactor $\prod_t\frac{1}{n_t!}(-ig_t)^{n_t}$ times the $\frac{1}{m_{t,k}!}$ that $\mathcal L_{\rm int}$ carries ([[§C6a.13 Feynman Rules for Polynomial Interactions#^def-c6a-13-1|Def. §C6a.13.1]], [[§C6a.13 Feynman Rules for Polynomial Interactions#^thm-c6a-13-4|Theorem §C6a.13.4]]). Label the legs: $\hat\phi_{(v,a)}$ is the $a$-th factor at vertex $v$. ^p5-1

2. **The order of the relabelling group.** $|G| = n!\,(4!)^n$ for $\phi^4$ ([[§CA.8 Pairings, Diagrams and Symmetry Factors#^def-ca-8-3|Def. §CA.8.3]]); in general $|G| = \prod_tn_t!\,\bigl(\prod_km_{t,k}!\bigr)^{n_t}$, legs of different kinds ($\phi$, $\phi^\dagger$; $\Phi$, $\phi$) never exchanged ([[§CA.9 Splitting Pairings and the Exponential Formula#^thm-ca-9-4|Theorem §CA.9.4]], 1, with its table: $\phi^3$: $n!\,(3!)^n$; $(\phi^\dagger\phi)^2$: $n!\,(2!\,2!)^n$; $\Phi\phi^2$: $n!\,(2!)^n$; a source: $n!$). When $\mathcal L_{\rm int}$ carries these factorials, $|G|$ is the inverse of the prefactor of step 1 apart from the couplings. ^p5-2

3. **Count the contractions that give the diagram, or find its stabilizer.** *Counting:* list the complete contractions producing the diagram as products of choices, external points first (which vertex, which leg), then the lines between vertices, then the loops, and multiply: $N_D$ ([[§C6a.9 Symmetry Factors in Practice#^ex-c6a-9-2|Example §C6a.9.2]] lists them for the melon). *Symmetry:* fix one labelled diagram $P$ and find the relabellings that map it to itself: every leg joined to an external point is fixed, and so is its vertex; what remains are flips of loops, permutations of parallel lines and of identical vertices ([[§CA.8 Pairings, Diagrams and Symmetry Factors#^thm-ca-8-4|Theorem §CA.8.4]]; [[§CA.9 Splitting Pairings and the Exponential Formula#^thm-ca-9-4|Theorem §CA.9.4]], 3). ^p5-3

4. **Divide.** $S = \dfrac{|G|}{N_D} = |\operatorname{Stab}(P)|$, orbit–stabilizer ([[§CA.8 Pairings, Diagrams and Symmetry Factors#^thm-ca-8-5|Theorem §CA.8.5]]; [[§CA.9 Splitting Pairings and the Exponential Formula#^thm-ca-9-4|Theorem §CA.9.4]], 2); the diagram is $\frac{1}{S}\prod_v(\text{vertex factor})\int\prod_{\text{lines}}D_F$, vertex factor $-ig_t\prod_km_{t,k}!$ ([[§C6a.8 Position-Space Feynman Rules#^thm-c6a-8-1|Theorem §C6a.8.1]], [[§C6a.13 Feynman Rules for Polynomial Interactions#^thm-c6a-13-2|Theorem §C6a.13.2]]). ^p5-4

5. **Shortcut: the product formula** (one kind of leg, unoriented lines). $S = |\Gamma_D|\cdot\prod_v2^{\ell(v)}\,\ell(v)!\cdot\prod_{\{u,w\}}m(u, w)!$: a 2 per loop, $\ell!$ for $\ell$ loops at one vertex, $m!$ for $m$ parallel lines, and the vertex permutations $\Gamma_D$ that keep all multiplicities ([[§C6a.9 Symmetry Factors in Practice#^thm-c6a-9-1|Theorem §C6a.9.1]]; the slides' rule, [[§C6a.9 Symmetry Factors in Practice#^rem-c6a-9-1|§C6a.9, Remark: The slides' general rule in this language]]). With oriented lines a loop cannot be flipped and only co-oriented parallel lines can be exchanged ([[§C6a.9 Symmetry Factors in Practice#^thm-c6a-9-2|Theorem §C6a.9.2]]). With the external particles of an amplitude held fixed only loops and parallel internal lines can contribute ("$\mathcal M$ has less symmetry than the Green's function": [[§C6c.5 Second Order꞉ Loops, Amputation and Symmetry Factors#^thm-c6c-5-4|Theorem §C6c.5.4]], [[§C6c.6 Feynman Rules for Amplitudes#^thm-c6c-6-3|Theorem §C6c.6.3]]; the one-loop $2 \to 2$ diagrams have $S = 2$, [[§C6c.5 Second Order꞉ Loops, Amputation and Symmetry Factors#^ex-c6c-5-1|Example §C6c.5.1]]). If every vertex $v$ carries at least $r_v - 1$ external lines, $S = 1$, and so for every tree with labelled external lines ([[§C6c.5 Second Order꞉ Loops, Amputation and Symmetry Factors#^thm-c6c-5-5|Theorem §C6c.5.5]], [[§C6c.5 Second Order꞉ Loops, Amputation and Symmetry Factors#^thm-c6c-5-6|Theorem §C6c.5.6]], [[§C6c.6 Feynman Rules for Amplitudes#^thm-c6c-6-2|Theorem §C6c.6.2]]). ^p5-5

6. **Check the counts.** The $N_D$ of all diagrams with the same external points and vertices add up to the number of pairings of the half-edges, $\sum_DN_D = (2k - 1)!!$ for $2k$ half-edges of one kind ([[§CA.8 Pairings, Diagrams and Symmetry Factors#^thm-ca-8-1|Theorem §CA.8.1]], [[§CA.8 Pairings, Diagrams and Symmetry Factors#^thm-ca-8-6|Corollary §CA.8.6]]): first order, two-point, $12 + 3 = 15 = 5!!$ ([[§C6a.7 Feynman Diagrams for Correlation Functions#^thm-c6a-7-3|Theorem §C6a.7.3]]); second-order vacuum, $24 + 72 + 9 = 105 = 7!!$ ([[§CA.8 Pairings, Diagrams and Symmetry Factors#^ex-ca-8-1|Example §CA.8.1]]). Steps 3–4 and step 5 must give the same $S$. ^p5-6

## Pitfalls

- **Loops at one vertex are permuted too**: two loops at a vertex give $2\cdot2\cdot2!$, not $2\cdot2$ ([[§C6a.9 Symmetry Factors in Practice#^cau-c6a-9-1|§C6a.9, Caution: Loops at one vertex are permuted too]]).
- **External points are never relabelled.** A vertex joined to an external point cannot move; in scattering diagrams the particles are labels, so $s$, $t$, $u$ are three diagrams with $S = 1$ each.
- **The $\frac{1}{m_{t,k}!}$ must be in $\mathcal L_{\rm int}$** for the prefactor to cancel against $|G|$; with $\mu\Phi\phi^2$ the vertex factor carries the $2!$ ([[§C6a.13 Feynman Rules for Polynomial Interactions#^thm-c6a-13-2|Theorem §C6a.13.2]]), with $\frac\lambda4(\phi^\dagger\phi)^2$ the group is $(2!\,2!)^n$, not $(4!)^n$.
- **Arrows forbid flips**: a $\phi$ leg is never exchanged with a $\phi^\dagger$ leg; QED's "S = 1" holds only for diagrams with external points ([[§C6a.9 Symmetry Factors in Practice#^cau-c6a-9-2|§C6a.9, Caution: "In QED the symmetry factor is always S = 1" holds for diagrams with external points]]).
- **Three S's**: the symmetry factor is not the $S$-matrix and not $S_{\rm id}$, the identical-particle factor of a cross section ([[§C6a.8 Position-Space Feynman Rules#^cau-c6a-8-1|§C6a.8, Caution: Three S's]]); the same list with the scalar bilinear $\mathsf S$ as a fourth entry is [[§C6b.1 The S-Matrix and the Invariant Amplitude#^cau-c6b-1-2|§C6b.1, Caution: Four S's]], cited in [[P7 From ℳ to σ or Γ|P7]].
- **Notation**: the problem sets write $k$ vertices and $N$ contractions; the vault $n$ vertices and $N_D$ ([[§C6a.9 Symmetry Factors in Practice#^ex-c6a-9-5|Example §C6a.9.5]]).
