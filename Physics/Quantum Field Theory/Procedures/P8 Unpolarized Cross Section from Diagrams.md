# P8 Unpolarized Cross Section from Diagrams

Turns a process with spin-$\frac12$ particles into its unpolarized cross section: from the diagrams to $i\mathcal M$, to the spin-averaged $|\mathcal M|^2$ as a function of invariants, to $d\sigma/d\Omega$ in a chosen frame. It is the general strategy of Lecture 19 (slide 26; PS p. 138), six steps; steps 1–2 are [[P6 Tree Amplitude from a Lagrangian]], steps 5–6 use [[P7 From ℳ to σ or Γ]], and steps 3–4, the square, the spin average and the traces, are new. Derivations are in the sections linked at each step; conventions in [[Larsen PHY 513]].

## Steps

1. **Draw all diagrams** with the desired initial and final particles, at the lowest order that connects them. Name the model and its vertices ([[§C8.6 Electron–Positron Annihilation꞉ the Amplitude and the Virtual Photon#^mod-c8-6-1|Model §C8.6.1]], [[§C8.4 Feynman Rules for QED#^thm-c8-4-3|Theorem §C8.4.3]]), count the vertices the process needs ([[§C8.4 Feynman Rules for QED#^thm-c8-4-2|Theorem §C8.4.2]], 1), and use the conservation of each species' fermion number to see which lines can meet ([[P6 Tree Amplitude from a Lagrangian#^p6-1|P6, step 1]], [[P6 Tree Amplitude from a Lagrangian#^p6-3|P6, step 3]]). ^p8-1

2. **Use the Feynman rules to find $i\mathcal M$** ([[§C8.4 Feynman Rules for QED#^thm-c8-4-4|Theorem §C8.4.4]]; [[P6 Tree Amplitude from a Lagrangian#^p6-4|P6, step 4]]–[[P6 Tree Amplitude from a Lagrangian#^p6-6|P6, step 6]]): each fermion line read against its arrow, external factors $u$, $\bar u$, $\bar v$, $v$ decided by which of $\hat\psi$, $\hat{\bar\psi}$ contains the operator matching the state ([[§C6d.2 Contractions with External Fermions#^thm-c6d-2-1|Theorem §C6d.2.1]]), $-ie\gamma^\mu$ at each vertex, $\frac{-ig_{\mu\nu}}{q^2}$ for each photon line with $q$ from momentum conservation; the $i\varepsilon$ dropped where the line cannot reach its pole ([[§C8.6 Electron–Positron Annihilation꞉ the Amplitude and the Virtual Photon#^thm-c8-6-3|Theorem §C8.6.3]], 3); the sign from the contractions ([[§C6d.3 Wick's Theorem with Fermions and the Contraction Rule#^thm-c6d-3-4|Theorem §C6d.3.4]]). ^p8-2

3. **Square the amplitude and average over spins.** Conjugate each bilinear by reversing it, $(\bar v\gamma^\mu u)^\ast = \bar u\gamma^\mu v$ ([[§C8.7 The Spin-Averaged Squared Amplitude#^thm-c8-7-1|Theorem §C8.7.1]]), with a new name for the summed index of $\mathcal M^\ast$ ([[§C8.7 The Spin-Averaged Squared Amplitude#^thm-c8-7-2|Theorem §C8.7.2]]). Sum over the final spins and average over the initial ones ([[§C8.7 The Spin-Averaged Squared Amplitude#^def-c8-7-1|Def. §C8.7.1]]; [[§C6b.3 The Cross Section#^def-c6b-3-5|Def. §C6b.3.5]]), and use the completeness relations $\sum_su\bar u = \slashed p + m$, $\sum_sv\bar v = \slashed p - m$ ([[§C5a.12 Normalization and Spin Sums#^thm-c5a-12-7|Theorem §C5a.12.7]], [[§C5a.12 Normalization and Spin Sums#^thm-c5a-12-8|Theorem §C5a.12.8]]) to turn each fermion line into a trace ([[§C8.7 The Spin-Averaged Squared Amplitude#^thm-c8-7-3|Theorem §C8.7.3]]). For external photons the corresponding step is the polarization sum ([[§C4.11 Vector-Field Propagators#^thm-c4-11-10|Theorem §C4.11.10]]; in the course with Compton scattering, planned). ^p8-3

4. **Evaluate the traces** with the Dirac algebra, not with explicit matrices: odd traces vanish, $\operatorname{tr}\gamma^\mu\gamma^\nu = 4g^{\mu\nu}$, the four-$\gamma$ formula ([[§C5a.14 Gamma-Matrix Technology#^thm-c5a-14-3|Theorem §C5a.14.3]]), the contraction identities where an index is summed across other $\gamma$'s ([[§C5a.14 Gamma-Matrix Technology#^thm-c5a-14-2|Theorem §C5a.14.2]]), and the $\gamma^5$ traces when they occur ([[§C5a.14 Gamma-Matrix Technology#^thm-c5a-14-4|Theorem §C5a.14.4]]). Contract the indices that the photon lines join. The result is $\frac14\sum_{\rm spins}|\mathcal M|^2$ in terms of dot products of four-momenta and masses, Lorentz invariant. ^p8-4

5. **Choose a reference frame** and draw the momenta in it. Express the four-momenta, and then the invariants, in terms of the energy, the scattering angle and the masses, using the mass-shell conditions of the external particles ([[§C8.6 Electron–Positron Annihilation꞉ the Amplitude and the Virtual Photon#^def-c8-6-2|Def. §C8.6.2]]; centre-of-mass frame: [[§C6b.3 The Cross Section#^def-c6b-3-6|Def. §C6b.3.6]], [[§C6b.4 Two-Body Scattering Kinematics#^thm-c6b-4-2|Theorem §C6b.4.2]]). State which masses are neglected. ^p8-5

6. **Compute the cross section**, taking phase space and kinematics into account: $\frac{d\sigma}{d\Omega} = \frac{1}{2E_A2E_B|\mathbf v_A - \mathbf v_B|}\cdot\frac{|\mathbf p_1|}{16\pi^2E_{\rm cm}}\cdot\frac14\sum|\mathcal M|^2$ in the centre-of-mass frame ([[§C6b.4 Two-Body Scattering Kinematics#^thm-c6b-4-4|Theorem §C6b.4.4]]; [[P7 From ℳ to σ or Γ#^p7-6|P7, step 6]], [[P7 From ℳ to σ or Γ#^p7-8|P7, step 8]]), with $e^4 = 16\pi^2\alpha^2$; integrate over the directions for $\sigma_{\rm tot}$ ([[§C6b.4 Two-Body Scattering Kinematics#^thm-c6b-4-5|Theorem §C6b.4.5]]; identical final particles: [[P7 From ℳ to σ or Γ#^p7-7|P7, step 7]]). Check the dimension, the threshold and the high-energy limit, and convert units if a number is wanted ([[P7 From ℳ to σ or Γ#^p7-9|P7, step 9]], [[P4 Restoring ħ and c]]). ^p8-6

## Pitfalls

- **$m_\mu$ is a mass.** The subscript names the muon and is never summed ([[§C8.7 The Spin-Averaged Squared Amplitude#^cau-c8-7-1|§C8.7, Caution: The subscript of the muon mass is not an index]]).
- **Rename the summed index** in $\mathcal M^\ast$ before multiplying; $q^4$ means $(q^2)^2$ ([[§C8.7 The Spin-Averaged Squared Amplitude#^thm-c8-7-2|Theorem §C8.7.2]]).
- **The factor of the average** is $\frac12$ for each initial spin-$\frac12$ particle, $\frac14$ for two; final spins are summed, not averaged ([[§C8.7 The Spin-Averaged Squared Amplitude#^def-c8-7-1|Def. §C8.7.1]]).
- **The order inside a trace** follows the fermion line; do not copy it from another line ([[§C8.7 The Spin-Averaged Squared Amplitude#^cau-c8-7-2|§C8.7, Caution: The order inside the muon trace on the slide]]).
- **$E$ and $E_{\rm cm}$.** In the centre-of-mass formulas $E$ is the energy of each particle, $E_{\rm cm} = 2E$ ([[§C8.8 The Unpolarized Cross Section for Muon Pair Production#^thm-c8-8-3|Theorem §C8.8.3]], 4).
- **A neglected mass is a choice**, to be stated; the invariant form holds for all masses ([[§C8.8 The Unpolarized Cross Section for Muon Pair Production#^rem-c8-8-1|§C8.8, Remark: Setting the electron mass to zero is a choice]]).
- **Sums over unobserved final states** (spins, colours) multiply the rate, not the amplitude ([[§C8.9 Quark–Antiquark Production and the Ratio R#^thm-c8-9-1|Theorem §C8.9.1]]).
- **Kinematics and dynamics.** The prefactor and the square root of $d\sigma/d\Omega$ come from the flux and the phase space; the theory is in $\frac14\sum|\mathcal M|^2$ ([[§C8.8 The Unpolarized Cross Section for Muon Pair Production#^rem-c8-8-2|§C8.8, Remark: Kinematics and dynamics]]).
