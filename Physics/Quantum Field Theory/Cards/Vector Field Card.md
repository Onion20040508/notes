# Vector Field Card

One-page reference for the free vector field: the massive (Proca) field and the photon in covariant gauges (canonical quantization, polarization vectors, states, gauge fixing, the Gupta–Bleuler condition, propagators); every formula is stated and derived in its home box in [[· C4 The Quantum Vector Field|C4 The Quantum Vector Field]] (the representation in [[· C3 Poincaré Symmetry and Particle States|C3]], the classical Maxwell theory in [[§C1.7 Relativistic Electrodynamics in Index Form|§C1.7]] and [[§C1.4 Gauge Fixing and the Two Polarizations|EM §C1.4]]), linked after it. Marks: ★ = beyond PHY 513 (§C4.2★–§C4.4★, Proca); *draft* = written ahead of the photon lectures (§C4.5–§C4.8). Conventions: $\hbar = c = 1$, Heaviside–Lorentz units, $g = \mathrm{diag}(+,-,-,-)$, $F^{\mu\nu} = \partial^\mu A^\nu - \partial^\nu A^\mu$; massive shell $p^0 = E_{\mathbf p} = \sqrt{\mathbf p^2 + m^2}$, massless $p^0 = |\mathbf p|$; $\xi = x - y$ in two-point functions, $\xi$ = gauge parameter in $\mathcal L_\xi$.

## 8. Pitfalls

- Index placement: the canonical pairs are $(A^i, \pi_i)$ with $\pi_i = -E^i$, or $(A_i, \pi^i)$ with $\pi^i = E^i$ — [[§C4.2★ The Proca Field#^cau-c4-2-1|§C4.2★, Caution: Index placement of the momentum across sources]]
- $\xi$ in the denominator here, Yu and PS; in the Electromagnetism Fermi example it multiplies (Landau $\xi \to 0$ here is $\xi \to \infty$ there) — [[§C4.5 The Maxwell Field, Gauge Symmetry and Gauge Fixing#^cau-c4-5-2|§C4.5, Caution: The gauge parameter ξ across the sources]]
- Names: Yu $e(p, \sigma)$, $b_{\mathbf p,\sigma}$, $\Delta_F^{\mu\nu}$; PS $\epsilon^r_\mu$, $a^r_{\mathbf p}$, $D_F^{\mu\nu}$ — [[§C4.5 The Maxwell Field, Gauge Symmetry and Gauge Fixing#^cau-c4-5-1|§C4.5, Caution: Names of polarization vectors and mode operators across the sources]], [[§C4.8 Vector-Field Propagators#^cau-c4-8-1|§C4.8, Caution: Names and signs of the vector propagator]]
- Yu's $\mathbf e(p, 1)$, $\mathbf e(p, 2)$ are undefined on the $z$-axis; no Condon–Shortley sign in $\varepsilon(p, \pm)$ — [[§C4.3★ Massive Polarization Vectors and the Plane-Wave Expansion#^cau-c4-3-1|§C4.3★, Caution: Phase conventions and the z-axis]]
- The Lorenz condition is a consequence for Proca, a choice for Maxwell, and false as an operator identity in the covariant photon theory — [[§C4.5 The Maxwell Field, Gauge Symmetry and Gauge Fixing#^rem-c4-5-4|§C4.5, Remark: The Lorenz condition: derived for Proca, chosen for Maxwell]], [[§C4.6 Covariant Quantization and the Indefinite Metric#^thm-c4-6-5|Theorem §C4.6.5]]
- Positive energy eigenvalues do not mean positive norms: $\vert\mathbf p, 0\rangle$ has $H$-eigenvalue $E_0^{(\gamma)} + E_{\mathbf p}$ and negative norm; $E_0^{(\gamma)}$ counts four polarizations — [[§C4.6 Covariant Quantization and the Indefinite Metric#^thm-c4-6-14|Theorem §C4.6.14]], [[§C4.6 Covariant Quantization and the Indefinite Metric#^thm-c4-6-10|Theorem §C4.6.10]]
- $\langle0\vert T\{A^\mu A^\nu\}\vert0\rangle$ of the massive field is not $-(g + \partial\partial/m^2)D_F$: a contact term $-\frac{i}{m^2}\delta^\mu_0\delta^\nu_0\delta^4$ remains — [[§C4.8 Vector-Field Propagators#^thm-c4-8-3|Theorem §C4.8.3]]
- $\sum\varepsilon^*_\mu\varepsilon_\nu \to -g_{\mu\nu}$ is a replacement valid only against amplitudes obeying the Ward identity — [[§C4.8 Vector-Field Propagators#^thm-c4-8-10|Theorem §C4.8.10]]
