# Vector Field Card

One-page reference for the free vector field: the massive (Proca) field and the photon in covariant gauges (canonical quantization, polarization vectors, states, gauge fixing, the Gupta–Bleuler condition, propagators); every formula is stated and derived in its home box in [[· C4 The Quantum Vector Field|C4 The Quantum Vector Field]] (the representation in [[· C3 Poincaré Symmetry and Particle States|C3]], the classical Maxwell theory in [[§C1a.7 Relativistic Electrodynamics in Index Form|§C1a.7]] and [[§C1.4 Gauge Fixing and the Two Polarizations|EM §C1.4]]), linked after it. Marks: ★ = beyond PHY 513 (§C4.2★–§C4.5★, Proca); *draft* = written ahead of the photon lectures (§C4.6–§C4.9). Conventions: $\hbar = c = 1$, Heaviside–Lorentz units, $g = \mathrm{diag}(+,-,-,-)$, $F^{\mu\nu} = \partial^\mu A^\nu - \partial^\nu A^\mu$; massive shell $p^0 = E_{\mathbf p} = \sqrt{\mathbf p^2 + m^2}$, massless $p^0 = |\mathbf p|$; $\xi = x - y$ in two-point functions, $\xi$ = gauge parameter in $\mathcal L_\xi$. Quantum operators carry a hat ($\hat A^\mu$, $\hat a_{\mathbf p,\lambda}$, $\hat b_{\mathbf p,\sigma}$, $\hat H$, $\hat{\mathbf P}$); the classical rows (Lagrangian, field equation, classical Hamiltonian and brackets, gauge transformations, procedure steps 1–3), the polarization vectors and the c-number propagators do not ([[§C2a.1 Canonical Quantization of Fields#^cau-c2a-1-1|§C2a.1, Caution: Eₚ, not ωₚ; π, not Π]]).

## 8. Pitfalls

- Index placement: the canonical pairs are $(A^i, \pi_i)$ with $\pi_i = -E^i$, or $(A_i, \pi^i)$ with $\pi^i = E^i$ — [[§C4.2★ The Proca Field#^cau-c4-2-1|§C4.2★, Caution: Index placement of the momentum across sources]]
- $\xi$ in the denominator here, Yu and PS; in the Electromagnetism Fermi example it multiplies (Landau $\xi \to 0$ here is $\xi \to \infty$ there) — [[§C4.6 The Maxwell Field, Gauge Symmetry and Gauge Fixing#^cau-c4-6-2|§C4.6, Caution: The gauge parameter ξ across the sources]]
- Names: Yu $e(p, \sigma)$, $\hat b_{\mathbf p,\sigma}$, $\Delta_F^{\mu\nu}$; PS $\epsilon^r_\mu$, $\hat a^r_{\mathbf p}$, $D_F^{\mu\nu}$ — [[§C4.6 The Maxwell Field, Gauge Symmetry and Gauge Fixing#^cau-c4-6-1|§C4.6, Caution: Names of polarization vectors and mode operators across the sources]], [[§C4.9 Vector-Field Propagators#^cau-c4-9-1|§C4.9, Caution: Names and signs of the vector propagator]]
- Yu's $\mathbf e(p, 1)$, $\mathbf e(p, 2)$ are undefined on the $z$-axis; no Condon–Shortley sign in $\varepsilon(p, \pm)$ — [[§C4.3★ Massive Polarization Vectors and Plane Waves#^cau-c4-3-1|§C4.3★, Caution: Phase conventions and the z-axis]]
- The Lorenz condition is a consequence for Proca, a choice for Maxwell, and false as an operator identity in the covariant photon theory — [[§C4.6 The Maxwell Field, Gauge Symmetry and Gauge Fixing#^rem-c4-6-4|§C4.6, Remark: The Lorenz condition: derived for Proca, chosen for Maxwell]], [[§C4.7 Covariant Quantization and the Indefinite Metric#^thm-c4-7-7|Theorem §C4.7.7]]
- Positive energy eigenvalues do not mean positive norms: $\vert\mathbf p, 0\rangle$ has $\hat H$-eigenvalue $E_0^{(\gamma)} + E_{\mathbf p}$ and negative norm; $E_0^{(\gamma)}$ counts four polarizations — [[§C4.7 Covariant Quantization and the Indefinite Metric#^thm-c4-7-17|Theorem §C4.7.17]], [[§C4.7 Covariant Quantization and the Indefinite Metric#^thm-c4-7-13|Theorem §C4.7.13]]
- $\langle0\vert T\{\hat A^\mu \hat A^\nu\}\vert0\rangle$ of the massive field is not $-(g + \partial\partial/m^2)D_F$: a contact term $-\frac{i}{m^2}\delta^\mu_0\delta^\nu_0\delta^4$ remains — [[§C4.9 Vector-Field Propagators#^thm-c4-9-3|Theorem §C4.9.3]]
- $\sum\varepsilon^*_\mu\varepsilon_\nu \to -g_{\mu\nu}$ is a replacement valid only against amplitudes obeying the Ward identity — [[§C4.9 Vector-Field Propagators#^thm-c4-9-10|Theorem §C4.9.10]]
