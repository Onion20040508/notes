# Spinor Field Card

One-page reference for the free Dirac field (γ matrices and the spinor Lorentz action, bilinears, the Dirac equation and its canonical structure, currents, plane-wave spinors, anticommutator quantization, fermion states, two-point functions, parity); every formula is stated and derived in its home box in [[· C5a Spinors and the Dirac Equation|C5a Spinors and the Dirac Equation]] and [[· C5b The Quantum Spinor Field|C5b The Quantum Spinor Field]] (the mathematics of Clifford algebras and spin groups in [[· CB Lie Groups, Lie Algebras and Representations|CB]], parity in [[· C9 Discrete Symmetries and Majorana Fields|C9]]), linked after it. Marks: *draft* = written ahead of the lectures (§C5b.5, §C5b.6, §C5b.8); ★ = beyond PHY 513. Conventions: $\hbar = c = 1$, $g = \mathrm{diag}(+,-,-,-)$, chiral (Weyl) basis, $p^0 = E_{\mathbf p} = \sqrt{\mathbf p^2 + m^2}$ on shell, $\xi = x - y$, $\tilde x = (t, -\mathbf x)$, $\tilde p = (p^0, -\mathbf p)$. Quantum operators carry a hat ($\hat\psi$, $\hat{\bar\psi} = \hat\psi^\dagger\gamma^0$, $\hat a^s_{\mathbf p}$, $\hat b^s_{\mathbf p}$, $\hat H$, $\hat{\mathbf P}$, $\hat Q$); the classical field of C5a, the spinors $u^s(p)$, $v^s(p)$, the matrices $\gamma^\mu$, $\Lambda_{1/2}$, $H_{\text{s.p.}}$ and the c-number two-point functions do not, so $u^{s\dagger}$ is a row spinor and $\hat a^{s\dagger}_{\mathbf p}$ a creation operator ([[§C2a.1 Canonical Quantization of Fields#^cau-c2a-1-1|§C2a.1, Caution: Eₚ, not ωₚ; π, not Π]], [[§C5b.1 Canonical Quantization of the Dirac Field#^cau-c5b-1-1|§C5b.1, Caution: Names for the Dirac field and its mode operators across sources]]). Scalar and vector counterparts: [[Scalar Field Card]], [[Vector Field Card]].
