**The question.** Do the laws of horizons mirror the laws of heat? Chapters C10–C12 found three facts in passing: the surface gravity κ is the same everywhere on the Schwarzschild, Reissner–Nordström and Kerr horizons; the Kerr mass obeys δM = (κ/8π)δA + Ω_HδJ; and the horizon area never decreased in any process computed. This chapter proves them in general: κ is constant on any stationary horizon when matter obeys the dominant energy condition (§C13.1); Smarr's formula M = κA/4π + 2Ω_HJ + Φ_HQ follows from Komar integrals, and with it the first law including charge (§C13.2); the area of the event horizon never decreases, which bounds the energy a merger can radiate (§C13.3); and extremality cannot be reached, while the area plays the role of an entropy S = A/4 (§C13.4). The analogy is formal until the Hawking temperature T = ħκ/2π, derived in C14, makes it physics.
