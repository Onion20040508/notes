**The question.** Why can gravity not simply be one more force field in special relativity, and what must replace Newton's theory? The chapter recasts Newtonian gravity as a field theory and shows where it collides with level B (§C1.1), fixes the conventions of level C (§C1.2), takes the equality of inertial and gravitational mass as a principle (§C1.3), derives from it that clocks in a gravitational field run at different rates, so that no global inertial frame exists (§C1.4), and shows that a metric with g₀₀ = −(1 + 2Φ), together with the rule that free bodies extremize proper time, reproduces Newtonian gravity (§C1.5). The mathematics this needs — manifolds, metrics, geodesics, curvature — is built in C2–C3; the field equations come in C5.
