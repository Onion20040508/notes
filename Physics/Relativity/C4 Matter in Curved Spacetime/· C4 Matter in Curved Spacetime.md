**The question.** What stands on the right-hand side of a gravitational field equation? C1 found that the Newtonian source ρ is one component of a tensor (moving dust has density γ²ρ₀), so the source must be a symmetric tensor built from matter. The chapter carries special-relativistic laws into curved spacetime by minimal coupling, the Einstein equivalence principle in tensor form, and says what it leaves open (§C4.1); builds the energy–momentum tensor from counting particles (§C4.2) and adds pressure (§C4.3); states what conservation means when spacetime curves, and when it yields conserved totals (§C4.4); and lists the positivity conditions on T that later theorems need (§C4.5).
