**The question.** C1 needed a tensor equation with a tensor source; C3 supplied curvature and C4 the source T with ∇·T = 0. Which equation relates them, and why that one? The chapter rewrites Poisson's equation in curvature language, shows that the naive guess R = 4πT fails and that G + Λg is forced (§C5.1); derives the equation from the Einstein–Hilbert action, every term of the variation written out (§C5.2); couples matter through the Hilbert energy–momentum tensor (§C5.3); explains diffeomorphism invariance as gauge freedom, the Bianchi identity as its Noether identity and the count of two degrees of freedom (§C5.4); fixes the coupling 8πG/c⁴ and derives the full weak-field metric in the Newtonian limit (§C5.5); and discusses the cosmological constant and why higher-curvature terms are excluded (§C5.6).
