**The question.** What is the field outside a spherical body, and how does it differ from Newton's? The chapter says what spherical symmetry means for a spacetime (§C6.1), solves the vacuum equations exactly and proves that the solution is the only one, whatever the body does inside (§C6.2), and then computes each classical test as a difference from Newton: orbits and the effective potential with its innermost stable orbit (§C6.3), the advance of Mercury's perihelion (§C6.4), the bending of light, twice the equivalence-principle value (§C6.5), and the redshift and the radar time delay (§C6.6) — each with the measurement, dated.
