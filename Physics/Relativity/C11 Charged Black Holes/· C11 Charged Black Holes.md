**The question.** What if the hole carries charge? Units here: G = c = 1 with Gaussian electromagnetism (μ₀ = 4π). The chapter couples Maxwell's field to gravity and finds that its energy curves spacetime (§C11.1), solves the spherical case exactly — the Reissner–Nordström metric, with two horizons r± = M ± √(M² − Q²) and a singularity that repels (§C11.2) — extends it through both horizons into an infinite chain of universes, and treats the extremal and naked cases (§C11.3), makes precise where the future stops being determined by the past (Cauchy surfaces and the Cauchy horizon at r₋, §C11.4), and defines electric and magnetic charge as surface integrals, including charge without charge on the Einstein–Rosen bridge (§C11.5). Astrophysical holes are nearly neutral; the point of the chapter is the new causal structure, which Kerr shares (C12).
