A stationary distribution stops changing. Is it equilibrium? Not necessarily: probability can circulate around a loop forever while every population stays fixed. Equilibrium is the stronger condition that every pair of opposite flows balances, detailed balance. This section defines it, tests it on a ring, gives Kolmogorov's criterion, and connects it to the Gibbs distribution of Unit 1.

**Setup.** A master equation $\dot{\mathbf p} = W\mathbf p$ ([[§C5.5 The Master Equation#^thm-c5-5-1|Theorem §C5.5.1]]) with stationary distribution $\boldsymbol\pi$, $W\boldsymbol\pi = 0$ ([[§C5.5 The Master Equation#^thm-c5-5-3|Theorem §C5.5.3]]); flows $F_{ij} = W_{ij}\pi_j$ and currents $J_{ij}$ of [[§C5.5 The Master Equation#^def-c5-5-1|Def. §C5.5.1]].

> [!remark] Remark: Rates first, or probabilities first
> In a dynamical model the rates $W_{ij}$ are given, and $\boldsymbol\pi$ follows by solving $W\boldsymbol\pi = 0$. When equilibrium statistical mechanics already predicts $\boldsymbol\pi$, the logic can run the other way: the known $\boldsymbol\pi$ constrains which rates are allowed ([[§C5.6 Detailed Balance#^thm-c5-6-3|Theorem §C5.6.3]]). Both are used.

^rem-c5-6-1

> [!definition] Definition §C5.6.1: Detailed Balance
> A stationary distribution $\boldsymbol\pi$ satisfies **detailed balance** if every pair of opposite flows cancels:
>
> $$
> W_{ij}\pi_j = W_{ji}\pi_i \quad\text{for every connected pair } i, j ,
> $$
>
> that is, $J_{ij} = 0$ on every link. Stationarity asks less: only that the currents into each state add to zero, $\dot p_i = \sum_{j\neq i}J_{ij} = 0$.
>
> *Source: Mao §5.5, eq. (5.40), box "Detailed balance and its physical meaning"*

^def-c5-6-1

> [!theorem] Theorem §C5.6.1: What Detailed Balance Means
> 1. Under detailed balance there are no stationary probability currents: the system fluctuates but does not circulate.
> 2. For states unchanged by microscopic time reversal, a stationary trajectory $j_0 \to j_1 \to \cdots \to j_n$ and its reverse are equally probable.
>
> It does not make the effective equation reversible: relaxation from a nonequilibrium start is still forward in time ([[§C5.5 The Master Equation#^thm-c5-5-2|Theorem §C5.5.2]]).
>
> *Model:* [[§C5.6 Detailed Balance#^def-c5-6-1|Def. §C5.6.1]]; *applies:* time-reversal-even states for part 2.
>
> *Source: Mao §5.5, box "Detailed balance and its physical meaning"*

^thm-c5-6-1

> [!derivation]- Derivation
> **Part 1.** $J_{ij} = W_{ij}\pi_j - W_{ji}\pi_i = 0$ is the definition.
>
> **Part 2.** In the stationary state the path weight is $\pi_{j_0}\prod_kT_{j_{k+1}j_k}(\Delta t)$, to leading order $\pi_{j_0}\prod_kW_{j_{k+1}j_k}\Delta t$ for the jumps. Detailed balance, $W_{ba}\pi_a = W_{ab}\pi_b$, applied jump by jump turns $\pi_{j_0}W_{j_1j_0}W_{j_2j_1}\cdots$ into $W_{j_0j_1}W_{j_1j_2}\cdots\pi_{j_n}$, the weight of the reversed path. Waiting factors are the same in both directions.

^der-c5-6-1

*Uses:* [[§C5.6 Detailed Balance#^def-c5-6-1|Def. §C5.6.1]], [[§C5.5 The Master Equation#^def-c5-5-1|Def. §C5.5.1]]

> [!example] Example §C5.6.1: A Ring with a Steady Current
> Three states in a ring, every clockwise rate $k_+$ and every counterclockwise rate $k_-$. The uniform $\pi_i = \tfrac13$ is stationary for any $k_\pm$: each state receives $(k_+ + k_-)/3$ and loses the same. The clockwise current on each link is
>
> $$
> J_\circlearrowright = \frac{k_+ - k_-}3 ,
> $$
>
> zero only for $k_+ = k_-$. For $k_+ \neq k_-$ the populations are fixed while probability circulates: a **nonequilibrium steady state**, which needs a driving force to sustain the bias. A two-state system cannot circulate; a network with a loop can.
>
> *Source: Mao §5.5, eq. (5.41) and Figure 5.6 · Lecture 11/3*

^ex-c5-6-1

> [!theorem] Theorem §C5.6.2: Kolmogorov's Cycle Criterion
> For positive rates on a connected network with every transition allowed in both directions, detailed balance holds exactly when, for every closed cycle $C$,
>
> $$
> \prod_{(j\to i)\in C}W_{ij} = \prod_{(j\to i)\in C}W_{ji} :
> $$
>
> the product of rates around the cycle is the same in both directions.
>
> *Model:* [[§C5.5 The Master Equation#^thm-c5-5-1|Theorem §C5.5.1]]; *applies:* positive rates, connected network.
>
> *Source: Mao §5.5, eq. (5.42) (statement) · proof written here*

^thm-c5-6-2

> [!derivation]- Derivation
> **Necessity.** Around a cycle $j_0 \to j_1 \to \cdots \to j_n = j_0$, detailed balance gives $W_{j_{k+1}j_k}/W_{j_kj_{k+1}} = \pi_{j_{k+1}}/\pi_{j_k}$; the product over the cycle telescopes to $\pi_{j_0}/\pi_{j_0} = 1$.
>
> **Sufficiency.** Fix a state $o$ and define $\pi_i \propto \prod_kW_{j_{k+1}j_k}/W_{j_kj_{k+1}}$ along any path from $o$ to $i$. Two paths differ by a cycle, whose product is $1$, so $\pi_i$ is well defined; by construction $W_{ij}\pi_j = W_{ji}\pi_i$ on every link, and summing over $j$ gives $W\boldsymbol\pi = 0$.

^der-c5-6-2

*Uses:* [[§C5.6 Detailed Balance#^def-c5-6-1|Def. §C5.6.1]]

> [!theorem] Theorem §C5.6.3: Thermal Local Detailed Balance
> For a system in contact with one heat bath at $T$ and no external drive, with time-reversal-even states of energies $E_i$, the stationary distribution is the Gibbs one of Unit 1, $\pi_i = e^{-\beta E_i}/Z$ ([[§C4a.5 The Canonical and Grand-Canonical Ensembles#^thm-c4a-5-2|Theorem §C4a.5.2]]), and microscopic reversibility requires the rates to obey
>
> $$
> \frac{W_{ij}}{W_{ji}} = \frac{\pi_i}{\pi_j} = e^{-\beta(E_i - E_j)} .
> $$
>
> This makes $W_{ij}\pi_j = W_{ji}\pi_i$, so the Gibbs probabilities are stationary. The ratio fixes $\boldsymbol\pi$ but not the speed: scaling every rate by one constant leaves $\boldsymbol\pi$ and changes the relaxation time. If a state lumps many microstates, its weight includes their multiplicity, or a free-energy factor.
>
> *Model:* one bath, no drive, nondegenerate resolved microstates; *applies:* the "equilibrium-first" reading of [[§C5.6 Detailed Balance#^rem-c5-6-1|§C5.6, Remark: Rates first, or probabilities first]].
>
> *Source: Mao §5.5, eqs. (5.43)–(5.44)*

^thm-c5-6-3

> [!derivation]- Derivation
> With the ratio given, $W_{ij}\pi_j = W_{ji}\frac{\pi_i}{\pi_j}\pi_j = W_{ji}\pi_i$: detailed balance holds, and summing over $j$ gives stationarity ([[§C5.6 Detailed Balance#^def-c5-6-1|Def. §C5.6.1]]). Scaling $W \to cW$ leaves $W\boldsymbol\pi = 0$ and scales every eigenvalue, hence every relaxation time, by $1/c$ ([[§C5.5 The Master Equation#^thm-c5-5-4|Theorem §C5.5.4]]).

^der-c5-6-3

*Uses:* [[§C5.6 Detailed Balance#^def-c5-6-1|Def. §C5.6.1]], [[§C4a.5 The Canonical and Grand-Canonical Ensembles#^thm-c4a-5-2|Theorem §C4a.5.2]], [[§C5.5 The Master Equation#^thm-c5-5-4|Theorem §C5.5.4]]

> [!caution] Caution: Two kinds of time reversal
> The complete $S + E$ dynamics can be microscopically reversible ([[§C0.1 Microscopic Reversibility#^thm-c0-1-2|Theorem §C0.1.2]]). The effective master equation need not have a valid backward evolution, even with detailed balance ([[§C5.5 The Master Equation#^thm-c5-5-2|Theorem §C5.5.2]]). Detailed balance is a third thing: a symmetry between forward and reversed fluctuations in a stationary equilibrium process. It does not undo relaxation. In QFT, "detailed balance" names yet another statement, the equality of a scattering rate and its time reverse from time-reversal invariance of the $S$-matrix ([[§C9.13 Time Reversal of the S-Matrix and Detailed Balance#^thm-c9-13-2|QFT Theorem §C9.13.2]]); it is one microscopic input behind thermal rates like those of [[§C5.6 Detailed Balance#^thm-c5-6-3|Theorem §C5.6.3]], not the same condition.
