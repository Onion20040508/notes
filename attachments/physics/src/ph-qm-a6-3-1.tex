% Figure for Quantum Mechanics §A6.3 (band schemes of a conductor, an insulator, an intrinsic semiconductor and n- and p-type semiconductors; schematic; after UP Vol. 3 Figs. 9.17, 9.20, 9.21 and Tipler Figs. 10-22, 10-27b, 10-28b).
