% Figure for Oscillations, Waves and Optics §B6.1 (two Gaussian wave packets with the same carrier k_c = 8 and widths sigma_x = 1 (top) and 2.5 (bottom): left, the packet Re f = exp(-x^2/2 sigma^2) cos(k_c x) with its envelope; right, its spectrum f~(k), a Gaussian of width sigma_k = 1/sigma_x around k_c; computed from the Gaussian rule of Theorem §B4.4.2).

  \begin{axis}[px, name=a, xticklabels={}, ylabel={$\operatorname{Re} f$}]
