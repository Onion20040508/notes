% Figure for Quantum Field Theory §C2.4 (Theorem §C2.4.2, the spectrum of the free real scalar field). Plane of one component P_z of the total momentum and the energy E, in units of m. Vacuum: the point (0,0). One-particle states: the mass hyperbola E = sqrt(P^2 + m^2). n-particle states with total momentum P fill E >= sqrt(P^2 + n^2 m^2) (threshold reached when all n move together with P/n each); the two-particle continuum is shaded, the three-particle threshold dotted. Adapted from the TikZ figure in the user's PHY 513 notes, Ch. 4 (fig:spectrum).

  xlabel={$P_z/m$}, ylabel={$E/m$}, xlabel style={anchor=north west}, ylabel style={anchor=south},
  xtick={-3,-2,-1,1,2,3}, ytick={1,2,3,4}, clip=false, axis on top,
  legend style={font=\scriptsize, draw=none, fill=none, at={(0.5,-0.13)}, anchor=north, legend columns=1}, legend cell align=left]

\addplot[figR, very thick, domain=-3.2:3.2, samples=80] {sqrt(x^2+1)};
\addlegendentry{one particle: $E=\sqrt{\mathbf P^{2}+m^{2}}$ (mass hyperbola)}
\addplot[figB, dashed, domain=-3.2:3.2, samples=80] {sqrt(x^2+4)};
\addlegendentry{two-particle threshold $\sqrt{\mathbf P^{2}+4m^{2}}$; continuum shaded}

\addlegendentry{three-particle threshold $\sqrt{\mathbf P^{2}+9m^{2}}$}
