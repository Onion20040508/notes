% Figure for Relativity §C8.4, Def. §C8.4.1-§C8.4.2 and Theorem §C8.4.2: background and ripple, and what an average over l keeps.
% Model curve (illustration, units arbitrary): g(z) = gbar(z) + h(z), gbar = 1 + 0.3 sin(z/LB), h = a cos(z/lb), a = 0.08,
% lb (reduced wavelength) = 0.05, LB = 2, averaging window: box of width l = 1.5 centred at z.

% <cos^2(z/lb)> = 1/2 + (lb/(2l)) sin(l/lb) cos(2z/lb); <gbar> = 1 + 0.3 (2 LB/l) sin(l/(2 LB)) sin(z/LB).
