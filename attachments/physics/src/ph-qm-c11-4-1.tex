% Figure for Quantum Mechanics §C11.4★ (Theorem §C11.4.1, Theorem §C11.4.3, Example §C11.4.1). (a) Spin correlation E(a,b) = <(sigma1.a)(sigma2.b)> against the angle eta between the analyzers: singlet -cos(eta) (Griffiths eq. (12.4)) versus the local "baseball" model of Griffiths Problem 12.3, -1 + 2 eta/pi; they agree at eta = 0, pi/2, pi. (b) Wigner's form of Bell's inequality for coplanar a, b with c bisecting (Sakurai eqs. (3.438)-(3.441)): left side sin^2(theta) (= sin^2(theta_ab/2), theta_ab = 2 theta) against the right side 2 sin^2(theta/2); violated (shaded) for 0 < theta < pi/2, by cos(theta)(1 - cos(theta)), largest (1/4) at theta = pi/3; at theta = pi/4: 0.500 > 0.293.
