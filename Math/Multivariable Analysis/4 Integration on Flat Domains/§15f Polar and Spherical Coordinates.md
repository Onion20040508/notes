## Polar and Spherical Coordinates

The coordinate changes $x = r\cos\theta$, $y = r\sin\theta$ in the plane and $x = \rho\sin\phi\cos\theta$, $y = \rho\sin\phi\sin\theta$, $z = \rho\cos\phi$ in space. In Chapter 4 they are the running example of a change of variables whose Jacobian ($r$ and $\rho^2\sin\phi$) vanishes at a point, and they turn rotationally symmetric integrals into one-variable problems.
