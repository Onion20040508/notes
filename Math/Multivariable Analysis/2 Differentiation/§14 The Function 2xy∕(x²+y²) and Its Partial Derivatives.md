## The Function 2xy∕(x²+y²) and Its Partial Derivatives

The function $f(x,y) = \dfrac{2xy}{x^2 + y^2}$ with $f(0,0) = 0$ vanishes on both axes, so both partials exist at the origin, yet $f$ has no limit there. In Chapter 2 it is the standard example separating "partials exist" from continuity and from differentiability.

Partials exist at the origin, but $f$ is not continuous there.
