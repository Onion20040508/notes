## Radial Functions

Functions of the distance to the origin alone: $r = \sqrt{x^2 + y^2}$ in the plane and $f(r)$ built from it. Rotational symmetry reduces their derivatives to one-variable calculus, and Laplace's equation for $f(r)$ becomes an ODE whose solution is the fundamental solution $\ln r$, singular at the origin.

The fields $(x, y)$ and $(x/r, y/r)$ have divergence $2$ and $1/r$.
