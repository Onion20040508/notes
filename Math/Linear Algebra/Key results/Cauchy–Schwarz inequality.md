## Connections
- Makes angles meaningful: $\cos\theta=\langle u,v\rangle/(\|u\|\|v\|)\in[-1,1]$ over $\R$. Gives [[Triangle inequality|6.17]].
- Physics: the Robertson uncertainty relation $\sigma_A\sigma_B\ge\tfrac12|\langle[A,B]\rangle|$ is Cauchy–Schwarz applied to $(A-\langle A\rangle)\psi$ and $(B-\langle B\rangle)\psi$.
- Analysis: the integral form [[§20 Inner Products and Norms#^ladr-6-16|6.16]](b) is Cauchy–Schwarz in $L^2$; Hölder's inequality for $L^p$, [[Hölder's Inequality|551 Thm. §34.5]], generalizes it from the exponents 2, 2 to any conjugate pair.
- Several-variable calculus: its equality case gives the direction of steepest ascent, [[§9 Directional Derivatives#^rem-9-3|452 Remark: Gradient and Maximum Rate of Change]] (from [[Directional Derivative Formula|452 Thm. §9.1]]).
- Proved again for arbitrary inner product spaces in [[§23 Cauchy–Schwarz and the Induced Norm#^thm-23-1|556 Thm. §23.1]]; Hölder's inequality ([[§17 Hölder's Inequality for Sequences#^thm-17-1|556 Thm. §17.1]]) generalizes its case for sequences from the exponents 2, 2 to any conjugate pair.
- **Also in [[Applied Linear Algebra]]:** [[§56 Inner Product Spaces#^thm-56-4|235 Thm. §56.4]] (real inner product spaces).
