## Connections
- Real/determinant version: [[§37 Determinants#^ladr-9-64|Cayley–Hamilton theorem (LADR 9.64)]]. Use: every power $T^k$ ($k\ge0$) is a polynomial in $T$ of degree $<\dim V$, and, when $T$ is invertible, so is $T^{-1}$ (the constant term of the characteristic polynomial is then nonzero, since $0$ is not an eigenvalue).
- Relation to the [[§15 The Minimal Polynomial#^ladr-5-24|minimal polynomial]] (5.24): the characteristic polynomial is a polynomial multiple of it ([[§31 Generalized Eigenspace Decomposition#^ladr-8-30|8.30]]).
