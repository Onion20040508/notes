> [!definition] 3.54 Transpose, Aᵗ
> The *transpose* $A^t$ of an $m$-by-$n$ matrix $A$ is the $n$-by-$m$ matrix with $(A^t)_{k,j}=A_{j,k}$: rows and columns are interchanged.

## Connections
- Swaps row rank and column rank (used in [[Column rank equals row rank (LADR 3.57)]]). Complex analogue with conjugation: [[Conjugate transpose, A∗]]; its map-level meaning is the dual map ([[Dual map, T′]]) or adjoint ([[Adjoint, T∗]]).
