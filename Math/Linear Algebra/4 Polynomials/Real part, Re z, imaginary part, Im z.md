> [!definition] 4.1 Real part, Re z, imaginary part, Im z
> For $z=a+bi$ with $a,b\in\R$: the *real part* is $\operatorname{Re}z=a$ and the *imaginary part* is $\operatorname{Im}z=b$. So $z=\operatorname{Re}z+(\operatorname{Im}z)\,i$.
