> [!definition] 4.2 Complex conjugate, z, absolute value, |z|
> For $z\in\C$:
> - the *complex conjugate* is $\bar z=\operatorname{Re}z-(\operatorname{Im}z)\,i$;
> - the *absolute value* is $|z|=\sqrt{(\operatorname{Re}z)^2+(\operatorname{Im}z)^2}$.

## Connections
- Properties: [[Properties of complex numbers]]. The conjugate is why complex inner products are conjugate-symmetric ([[Inner product]]) and why adjoints involve conjugate transposes ([[Conjugate transpose, A∗]]).
