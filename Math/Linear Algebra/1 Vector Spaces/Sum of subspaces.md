> [!definition] 1.36 Sum of subspaces
> For subspaces $V_1,\dots,V_m$ of $V$,
> $$
> V_1+\dots+V_m=\{v_1+\dots+v_m : v_k\in V_k\}.
> $$

## Connections
- It is the smallest subspace containing all $V_k$: [[Sum of subspaces is the smallest containing subspace]]. When representations are unique: [[Direct sum, ⊕]].
