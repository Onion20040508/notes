> [!definition] 5.1 Operator
> An *operator* is a linear map from a vector space to itself; $\Lin(V)=\Lin(V,V)$.

> [!remark] Why operators get a richer theory
> Operators can be composed with themselves, so powers $T^m$ and polynomials $p(T)$ make sense (see [[Multiplicative properties]]). If $V=V_1\oplus\dots\oplus V_m$ with each $V_k$ invariant, understanding $T$ reduces to understanding each $T|_{V_k}$ ([[Invariant subspace]]).

## Connections
- Physics: observables and Hamiltonians are operators; their spectral theory is Chapters 5–7.
