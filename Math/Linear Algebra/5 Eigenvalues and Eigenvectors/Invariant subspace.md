> [!definition] 5.2 Invariant subspace
> For $T\in\Lin(V)$, a subspace $U$ of $V$ is *invariant under $T$* if $Tu\in U$ for every $u\in U$; equivalently $T|_U$ is an operator on $U$.

> [!remark] Examples
> $\{0\}$, $V$, $\nullsp T$ and $\range T$ are always invariant (Axler 5.4). More generally $\nullsp p(T)$ and $\range p(T)$ are ([[Null space and range of p(T) are invariant under T]]).

## Connections
- One-dimensional invariant subspaces are exactly eigenvector lines: [[Eigenvalue]], [[Eigenvector]].
- Restriction and minimal polynomials: [[Minimal polynomial of a restriction operator]]. Physics: symmetry sectors are invariant subspaces of the Hamiltonian.
