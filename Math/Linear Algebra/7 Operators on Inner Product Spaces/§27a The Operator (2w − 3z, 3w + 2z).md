The operator $T(w,z)=(2w-3z,\ 3w+2z)$, with $\mathcal{M}(T)=\begin{pmatrix}2&-3\\3&2\end{pmatrix}$, appears twice in Chapter 7. It is normal but not self-adjoint; over $\C$ it has an orthonormal basis of eigenvectors ([[Complex spectral theorem|7.31]]), and over $\R$ it has no eigenvalues.
