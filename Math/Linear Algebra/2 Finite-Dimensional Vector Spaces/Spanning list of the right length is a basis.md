> [!theorem] 2.42 Spanning list of the right length is a basis
> If $V$ is finite-dimensional, every spanning list in $V$ of length $\dim V$ is a basis of $V$.

> [!proof]-
> Reduce the list to a basis by [[Every spanning list contains a basis]]. Every basis has length $\dim V$ ([[Basis length does not depend on basis]]), so nothing was removed.

## Uses (in the proof)
- [[Every spanning list contains a basis]] (2.30)
- [[Basis length does not depend on basis]] (2.34)
