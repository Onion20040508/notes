> [!definition] 2.35 Dimension, dim V
> The *dimension* of a finite-dimensional vector space $V$, written $\dim V$, is the length of any basis of $V$.

> [!remark] Examples
> $\dim\F^n=n$; $\dim\Poly_m(\F)=m+1$. The field matters: $\dim_\C\C=1$ but $\dim_\R\C=2$.
