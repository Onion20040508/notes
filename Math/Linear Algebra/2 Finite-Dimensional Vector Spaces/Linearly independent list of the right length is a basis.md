> [!theorem] 2.38 Linearly independent list of the right length is a basis
> If $V$ is finite-dimensional, every linearly independent list in $V$ of length $\dim V$ is a basis of $V$.

> [!proof]-
> Extend the list to a basis by [[Every linearly independent list extends to a basis]]. Every basis has length $\dim V$ ([[Basis length does not depend on basis]]), so nothing was added.
