> [!definition] 2.10 Polynomial, P(F)
> A function $p:\F\to\F$ is a *polynomial with coefficients in $\F$* if there are $a_0,\dots,a_m\in\F$ with
> $$
> p(z)=a_0+a_1z+\dots+a_mz^m \quad\text{for all } z\in\F .
> $$
> $\mathcal{P}(\F)$ denotes the set of all such polynomials.

> [!remark] Structure
> $\Poly(\F)$ is a subspace of $\F^\F$ (functions $\F\to\F$). The coefficients are determined by the function; this is proved later ([[Degree m implies at most m zeros]]), and it is what makes [[Degree of a polynomial, deg p]] well defined.
