## Discrete and Indiscrete Spaces

$\mathbb{Z}$ with the discrete metric is closed and bounded but not compact. The product of $\mathbb{Z}_+$ with an indiscrete two-point space is limit point compact but not compact. The discrete subspace $\{1/n\}$ of $\mathbb{R}$ is locally compact but not compact, and its one-point compactification is the compact space $\{0\} \cup \{1/n\}$ of [[§15 Compact Spaces#^ex-15-4|the compactness example]].
