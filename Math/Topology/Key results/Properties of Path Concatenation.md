## Connections
- **Group axioms.** Restricted to loops at x₀, these are the group axioms of π₁ ([[§23 The Fundamental Group#^rem-23-1|remark after Definition §23.2]]). On arbitrary paths they only give a groupoid ([[§22 Homotopy of Paths#^rem-22-10|What These Properties Mean]]). They are stated on classes, which first requires [[§22 Homotopy of Paths#^prop-22-4|Proposition §22.4]].
- **Proof.** Each identity is witnessed by an explicit reparametrizing homotopy of the square, continuous by the [[Pasting Lemma]].
- **Used for.** It is used in [[Basepoint Independence of π₁]] and [[§23 The Fundamental Group#^lem-23-1|Paths in Simply Connected Spaces]] (§23.1), in the injectivity step of [[Properties of the Lifting Correspondence]], and in the telescoping product in [[§27 The Fundamental Group of Sⁿ#^thm-27-1|Generation by Open Cover]] (§27.1).
