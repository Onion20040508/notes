## Connections
- **Intuition.** It makes precise the idea that the closure consists of the points reachable by limits from A ([[Topology §7 Interior and Closure#^rem-7-2|Room to Wiggle and Reachable by Limits]]).
- **Immediate consequences.** [[Topology §7 Interior and Closure#^thm-7-4|Closure and Limit Points]] (§7.4) gives closure = A ∪ A′, and [[Topology §7 Interior and Closure#^cor-7-5|Corollary §7.5]] says A is closed iff it contains its limit points. It also drives (1) ⇒ (2) in [[Equivalent Conditions for Continuity]].
- **Metric spaces.** With sequences it becomes the [[Topology §11 Metric Topology#^lem-11-8|Sequence Lemma]] (§11.8): a limit of points of A lies in the closure, with the converse when X is metrizable. The MATH 451 counterpart is [[Single Variable Analysis §13 Some Topological Concepts in Metric Spaces#^prop-13-6|Sequential Characterization of the Closure]].
