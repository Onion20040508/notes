## Connections
- **Method.** To compute π₁(B), find a simply connected covering space; then π₁(B) is in bijection with a fiber ([[§24a Lifting and the Fundamental Group of the Circle#^rem-24-12|Why This Matters]]). In particular the number of sheets equals |π₁(B)| ([[§24 Covering Spaces#^rem-24-1|Why Copies Compute π₁]]).
- **Applications.** [[Fundamental Group of the Circle]] (fiber ℤ); [[§28 Fundamental Group of Some Surfaces#^thm-28-2|π₁(P²) ≅ ℤ/2ℤ]] and [[§28 Fundamental Group of Some Surfaces#^thm-28-3|π₁(Pⁿ) for all n]], using the two-point fibers of Sⁿ → Pⁿ and [[Sⁿ is Simply Connected for n ≥ 2]].
- **Only a bijection of sets.** The group structure must be checked separately, as in step 3 of the [[§24a Lifting and the Fundamental Group of the Circle#^pf-24-10|proof that π₁(S¹) ≅ ℤ]]. If E is not simply connected, counting sheets does not give |π₁(B)|.
- **Group-theoretic reading.** φ is the [[§27 Orbits#^def-27-1|orbit]] map of e₀ for the monodromy [[§25 Actions#^def-25-1|action]] of π₁(B, b₀) on the fibre; when E is simply connected the [[§26 Stabilizers and Fixed Points#^def-26-1|stabilizer]] is trivial and the bijection is the orbit bijection of [[§30 Orbit–Stabilizer#^prop-30-2|493 Prop. §30.2]] (hub [[Orbit–Stabilizer Theorem]]).
