## Connections
- **Method.** To compute π₁(B), find a simply connected covering space; then π₁(B) is in bijection with a fiber ([[Topology §24 Covering Spaces#^rem-24-12|Why This Matters]]). In particular the number of sheets equals |π₁(B)| ([[Topology §24 Covering Spaces#^rem-24-1|Why Copies Compute π₁]]).
- **Applications.** [[Fundamental Group of the Circle]] (fiber ℤ); [[Topology §28 Fundamental Group of Some Surfaces#^thm-28-2|π₁(P²) ≅ ℤ/2ℤ]] and [[Topology §28 Fundamental Group of Some Surfaces#^thm-28-3|π₁(Pⁿ) for all n]], using the two-point fibers of Sⁿ → Pⁿ and [[Sⁿ is Simply Connected for n ≥ 2]].
- **Only a bijection of sets.** The group structure must be checked separately, as in step 3 of the [[Topology §24 Covering Spaces#^pf-24-10|proof that π₁(S¹) ≅ ℤ]]. If E is not simply connected, counting sheets does not give |π₁(B)|.
