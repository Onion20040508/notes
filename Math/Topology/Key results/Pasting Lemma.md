## Connections
- **Proof.** It uses the closed-set criterion (3) of [[Equivalent Conditions for Continuity]], together with the fact that a set closed in a closed subspace is closed in X ([[§7 Closed Sets and Limit Points#^thm-7-3|Theorem §7.3]]).
- **Typical use.** It gives continuity of piecewise-defined maps, first |x| ([[§10 Continuous Functions#^ex-10-6|Example §10.6]]). In homotopy theory it covers path products f ∗ g ([[§28 Homotopy of Paths#^rem-28-6|Well-Definedness]]), transitivity of ≃ ([[§28 Homotopy of Paths#^lem-28-3|Lemma §28.3]]) and every homotopy in [[Properties of Path Concatenation]].
- **Covering spaces.** It glues finitely many local lifts in [[Path Lifting Lemma]] and [[Homotopy Lifting Lemma]], and builds the lift of f ∗ g in [[Fundamental Group of the Circle]].
