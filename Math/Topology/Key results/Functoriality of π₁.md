## Connections
- **Immediate consequence.** [[§23 The Fundamental Group#^cor-23-6|π₁ is a Topological Invariant]] (§23.6): homeomorphic spaces have isomorphic π₁, so non-isomorphic groups prove spaces non-homeomorphic ([[§23 The Fundamental Group#^rem-23-4|remark after §23.6]], [[§28 Fundamental Group of Some Surfaces#^cor-28-6|Four Topologically Distinct Surfaces]]).
- **Retractions.** Applied to r ∘ j = id it gives j∗ injective and r∗ surjective ([[§26 Deformation Retracts and Homotopy Type#^prop-26-3|Algebraic Properties of Retractions]]). This is the engine of the [[No-Retraction Theorem]] and of step 2 of [[Fundamental Theorem of Algebra (topological proof)]].
- **Up to homotopy.** Combined with [[§26 Deformation Retracts and Homotopy Type#^lem-26-1|Homotopic Maps and Induced Homomorphisms]], it yields [[Deformation Retract Induces Isomorphism on π₁]] and [[§26 Deformation Retracts and Homotopy Type#^thm-26-15|Homotopy Equivalence Induces Isomorphism on π₁]].
- **Transport.** A π₁ computation made once transfers along any homeomorphism ([[§23 The Fundamental Group#^rem-23-5|Naturality: Structure Transports Across Homeomorphisms]]), e.g. between S¹ ⊂ ℂ and S¹ ⊂ ℝ² ([[§9 Continuous Functions#^ex-9-5|Example §9.5]]).
- **Same idea elsewhere.** Differentials of smooth maps compose the same way, (G ∘ F)_* = G_* ∘ F_*: [[Chain Rule for Differentials]] (591 Thm. §26.6).
