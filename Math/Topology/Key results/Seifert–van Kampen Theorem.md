## Connections
- **Upgrades** [[§27 The Fundamental Group of Sⁿ#^thm-27-1|Generation by Open Cover]] (§27.1), which is its surjectivity half, from generators to the exact group. The relations come from loops in A ∩ B seen from both sides ([[§29 The Seifert–van Kampen Theorem#^rem-29-2|What N Does]]). The hard direction uses the [[Lebesgue Number Lemma]].
- **Special case.** When A ∩ B is simply connected, the [[§29 The Seifert–van Kampen Theorem#^cor-29-2|Free Product Formula]] applies. It gives [[§29 The Seifert–van Kampen Theorem#^ex-29-2|π₁(S¹ ∨ S¹) ≅ ℤ ∗ ℤ]], completing the non-abelian argument of [[§28 Fundamental Group of Some Surfaces#^thm-28-4|Theorem §28.4]].
- **Recomputations.** It recovers [[§29 The Seifert–van Kampen Theorem#^ex-29-1|π₁(Sⁿ) = 0]], [[§29 The Seifert–van Kampen Theorem#^ex-29-4|π₁(T²) ≅ ℤ × ℤ]] and [[§29 The Seifert–van Kampen Theorem#^ex-29-5|π₁(P²) ≅ ℤ/2ℤ]], found earlier by [[Sⁿ is Simply Connected for n ≥ 2]], the product formula and covering spaces.
- **Surfaces.** [[§29 The Seifert–van Kampen Theorem#^thm-29-3|Fundamental Group of a Polygonal Surface]] (§29.3): if all vertices are identified to one point, π₁ = ⟨a₁, …, a_k | w⟩, where w is the boundary word.
