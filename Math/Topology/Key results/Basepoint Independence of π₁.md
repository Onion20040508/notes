## Connections
- **Immediate consequence.** For path-connected X the isomorphism type of π₁(X, x₀) does not depend on x₀ ([[Topology §23 The Fundamental Group#^cor-23-3|Corollary §23.3]]); this is why simple connectivity can be checked at a single basepoint and why one writes π₁(S¹) ≅ ℤ without one ([[Topology §24 Covering Spaces#^rem-24-13|remark in §24]]).
- **Not canonical.** The isomorphism α̂ depends on the path α: a different path changes it by conjugation, so all choices agree when π₁ is abelian ([[Topology §23 The Fundamental Group#^rem-23-3|remark after Corollary §23.3]]).
- **Moving basepoints.** α̂ is the correction term in [[Topology §26 Deformation Retracts and Homotopy Type#^lem-26-14|Homotopic Maps and π₁: General Case]] (§26.14), k∗ = α̂ ∘ h∗, which is what proves [[Topology §26 Deformation Retracts and Homotopy Type#^thm-26-15|Homotopy Equivalence Induces Isomorphism on π₁]] (§26.15).
- **Proof pattern.** The group identities come from [[Properties of Path Concatenation]]; bijectivity is shown by exhibiting the two-sided inverse β̂ for the reverse path, the technique of [[Topology §21 Algebra Prerequisites꞉ Groups#^rem-21-4|the remark after Proposition §21.4]].
