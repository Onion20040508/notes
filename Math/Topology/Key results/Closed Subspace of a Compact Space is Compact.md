## Connections
- **Partner result.** It pairs with [[Compact Subspace of a Hausdorff Space is Closed]] ([[§15 Compact Spaces#^rem-15-4|Summary: Compact and Closed]]). In a compact Hausdorff space the two together say that a subset is closed exactly when it is compact.
- **Closedness is needed.** (0, 1] is a non-closed subspace of the compact interval [0, 1] and is not compact ([[§15 Compact Spaces#^ex-15-3|Example §15.3]]).
- **Immediate uses.** It is step 1 of [[Bijection from Compact to Hausdorff is a Homeomorphism]] and the last step of [[Heine–Borel Theorem]]: a closed bounded set is a closed subset of a compact box ([[§15 Compact Spaces#^rem-15-10|Filling the Gap: Balls vs. Boxes]]).
- **Later.** It is used in [[§20 Normal Spaces#^thm-20-2|Every Compact Hausdorff Space is Normal]] (§20.2) and throughout local compactness, e.g. [[§17 Local Compactness#^thm-17-5|One-Point Compactification]] (§17.5).
