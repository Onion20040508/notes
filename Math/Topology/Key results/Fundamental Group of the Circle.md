## Connections
- **Method.** Since ℝ is simply connected, [[Properties of the Lifting Correspondence]] makes [f] ↦ f̃(1) a bijection onto the fiber ℤ. The translation p(x + m) = p(x) makes it a homomorphism, and the standard loop is a generator ([[§32 Lifting and the Fundamental Group of the Circle#^thm-32-6|The Generator of π₁(S¹)]]).
- **Applications.** It gives the [[No-Retraction Theorem]] and hence the [[Brouwer Fixed Point Theorem]]. It also gives [[Fundamental Theorem of Algebra (topological proof)]], where z ↦ zⁿ induces multiplication by n.
- **Further computations.** [[§29 The Fundamental Group#^cor-29-8|π₁(T²) ≅ ℤ × ℤ]] (§29.8), π₁(ℝ² ∖ {0}) ≅ ℤ by deformation retraction ([[§35 Deformation Retracts and Homotopy Type#^ex-35-6|Example §35.6]]), and the figure eight via van Kampen ([[§39 The Seifert–van Kampen Theorem#^ex-39-2|Example §39.2]]).
- **Contrast with n ≥ 2.** The open-cover argument that proves [[Sⁿ is Simply Connected for n ≥ 2]] fails for S¹ because S¹ minus two points is disconnected ([[§37 The Fundamental Group of Sⁿ#^rem-37-3|Why n ≥ 2 is Essential]]). The difference between π₁(ℝ² ∖ {0}) and π₁(ℝ³ ∖ {0}) is what shows ℝ² ≇ ℝ³ ([[§29 The Fundamental Group#^rem-29-2|What π₁ Cannot See]]).
- **The group.** ℤ is the infinite cyclic group, classified in [[Classification of Cyclic Groups]] ([[§17 Cyclic Groups#^thm-17-1|493 Thm. §17.1]]); the standard loop is a generator in the sense of [[§4 Subgroups#^def-4-8|493 Def. §4.8]].
- **Also in [[Complex Variables]]:** [[§93 Argument Principle#^thm-93-4|342 Thm. §93.4]] (the argument principle computes the winding number of f(C) around 0 as the number of zeros minus poles; complex-variables version with worked examples).
