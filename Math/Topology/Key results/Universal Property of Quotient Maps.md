## Connections
- **Immediate consequence.** [[§13 Quotient Topology#^cor-13-4|Induced Bijection from Quotient]] (§13.4): a continuous surjection g induces a homeomorphism X* ≅ Z exactly when g is a quotient map. The hypothesis that g is constant on fibers is exactly what well-definedness needs ([[§13 Quotient Topology#^rem-13-7|Constant on Fibers]]).
- **Toolkit.** It is Theorem C of [[§13 Quotient Topology#^rem-13-8|The Quotient Space Toolkit]]. It is used together with [[Bijection from Compact to Hausdorff is a Homeomorphism]] (to recognize quotient maps) and [[§13 Quotient Topology#^thm-13-5|Product of Quotient Maps]].
- **Typical use.** Work upstairs, check the gluing, descend ([[§26 Algebra Prerequisites꞉ Groups#^rem-II-2|The Role of the Quotient Space Toolkit in Part II]]). A nullhomotopy descends through the cone map to an extension over the ball ([[§32 Lifting and the Fundamental Group of the Circle#^lem-32-7|Equivalent Conditions for Nullhomotopy]], [[§34 Retractions and Fixed Points#^lem-34-8|Generalized Nullhomotopy Lemma]]). A deformation retraction of the punctured square descends to T² ∖ {pt} ([[§35 Deformation Retracts and Homotopy Type#^ex-35-8|Example §35.8]]).
- **Dual picture.** Lifting through a covering map has the same triangle with the arrows through p reversed ([[§32 Lifting and the Fundamental Group of the Circle#^rem-32-6|Connection to Quotient Maps]]).
- **Algebraic counterparts.** Maps out of ℤ/nℤ that are constant on [[§6 Divisibility and Congruence#^def-6-4|residue classes]] descend the same way ([[Descent Lemma]], [[§7 The Group ℤ∕nℤ#^lem-7-2|493 Lemma §7.2]]), and a homomorphism factors through G/Ker α ([[First Isomorphism Theorem for Groups]], [[§41 The First and Second Isomorphism Theorems#^thm-41-1|493 Thm. §41.1]]).
