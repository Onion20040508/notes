## Connections
- **Immediate consequence.** [[§12 Quotient Topology#^cor-12-4|Induced Bijection from Quotient]] (§12.4): a continuous surjection g induces a homeomorphism X* ≅ Z exactly when g is a quotient map. The hypothesis that g is constant on fibers is exactly what well-definedness needs ([[§12 Quotient Topology#^rem-12-7|Constant on Fibers]]).
- **Toolkit.** It is Theorem C of [[§12 Quotient Topology#^rem-12-8|The Quotient Space Toolkit]]. It is used together with [[Bijection from Compact to Hausdorff is a Homeomorphism]] (to recognize quotient maps) and [[§12 Quotient Topology#^thm-12-5|Product of Quotient Maps]].
- **Typical use.** Work upstairs, check the gluing, descend ([[§21 Algebra Prerequisites꞉ Groups#^rem-II-2|The Role of the Quotient Space Toolkit in Part II]]). A nullhomotopy descends through the cone map to an extension over the ball ([[§24 Covering Spaces#^lem-24-12|Equivalent Conditions for Nullhomotopy]], [[§26 Deformation Retracts and Homotopy Type#^lem-26-8|Generalized Nullhomotopy Lemma]]). A deformation retraction of the punctured square descends to T² ∖ {pt} ([[§26 Deformation Retracts and Homotopy Type#^ex-26-9|Example §26.9]]).
- **Dual picture.** Lifting through a covering map has the same triangle with the arrows through p reversed ([[§24 Covering Spaces#^rem-24-6|Connection to Quotient Maps]]).
- **Algebraic counterparts.** Maps out of ℤ/nℤ that are constant on [[§6 Divisibility and Congruence#^def-6-4|residue classes]] descend the same way ([[Descent Lemma]], [[§7 The Group ℤ∕nℤ#^lem-7-2|493 Lemma §7.2]]), and a homomorphism factors through G/Ker α ([[First Isomorphism Theorem for Groups]], [[§41 The First and Second Isomorphism Theorems#^thm-41-1|493 Thm. §41.1]]).
