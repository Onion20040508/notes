The real line $\mathbb{R}_\ell$ with basis the half-open intervals $[a,b)$. It is strictly finer than the standard topology but still well behaved (first-countable, separable, normal), and it is the standard example of a space that is separable without being second-countable. Its uses in MATH 590:

- Definition: the basis $\{[a,b)\}$ ([[Topology §2 Basis for a Topology#^ex-2-3|§2]])
- $\mathbb{R}_\ell$ is strictly finer than $\mathbb{R}_{\text{std}}$ ([[Topology §2 Basis for a Topology#^ex-2-5|§2]])
- The identity $\mathbb{R}_\ell \to \mathbb{R}$ is continuous, but $\mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}_\ell$ is not ([[Topology §9 Continuous Functions#^ex-9-2|§9]])
- $\mathbb{R}_\ell$ is first-countable ([[Topology §18 Countability Axioms#^ex-18-2|§18]])
- $\mathbb{R}_\ell$ is separable but not second-countable ([[Topology §18 Countability Axioms#^pf-18-4|§18]])
- $\mathbb{R}_\ell$ is normal ([[Topology §19 Separation Axioms#^ex-19-2|§19]])
