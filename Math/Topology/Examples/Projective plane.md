The projective plane $P^2 = S^2/(x \sim -x)$, equivalently the disk with antipodal boundary points identified. It is the basic example of a space with finite non-trivial fundamental group: $\pi_1(P^2) \cong \mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}$, computed first from the double cover $S^2 \to P^2$ and again by van Kampen.
