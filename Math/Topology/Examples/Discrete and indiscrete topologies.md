The two extreme topologies on a set: the discrete topology, in which every subset is open, and the indiscrete (trivial) topology, in which only $\emptyset$ and $X$ are open. They are the quickest test cases for any definition: discrete spaces are Hausdorff, totally disconnected, and compact only when finite; indiscrete spaces with more than one point are connected and compact but not even $T_1$.
