# Course Log

The notes are arranged in logical order, not in the order of the lectures. This log records when each part was covered in MATH 556 (Fall 2026) and where it now appears, reconstructed from the `% Lecture N (…)` banners of the source tex, from the lecture mentions in the text (“(Lecture 5.)”, “the picture drawn in Lecture 3”, “given at the end of Lecture 3”), and from the board order of Lecture 4 recorded at the head of Chapter 3. The banners are sometimes placed by topic rather than chronologically: Chapter 3 was reordered so that “complete” comes before “completion”, so a lecture's material can be spread over several sections. Rows marked (approx.) are inferred.

**Course facts.** Instructor: Sijue Wu. Text: Lax, *Functional Analysis* (Chapters 1, 3, 5, 6 and 15), available electronically through the library; see [[Functional Analysis Lax Concordance]]. Lectures: Tuesday and Thursday. Grading: Homework/Quizzes 30%; one in-class midterm (Thursday, October 15) and one final (Friday, December 11, 6–8 pm, announced in Lecture 10; Wu asked the class whether the date works). Quizzes, if given, are unannounced and drawn from homework problems. Attendance required. Prerequisites: linear algebra (mandatory), some complex analysis, advanced calculus; Lebesgue theory not assumed.
