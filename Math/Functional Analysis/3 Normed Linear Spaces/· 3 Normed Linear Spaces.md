# 3 Normed Linear Spaces
↑ [[Functional Analysis]]

So far $X$ has had only linear structure, and the [[§4 Statement and Motivation#^thm-4-2|Hahn–Banach theorem]] is the most one can say. The next layer of structure is a notion of length. With it come distance, convergence, Cauchy sequences, and completeness, and the objects of the course ([[§11 Completeness#^def-11-2|Banach spaces]]) can finally be defined. This lecture follows Lax's book.
