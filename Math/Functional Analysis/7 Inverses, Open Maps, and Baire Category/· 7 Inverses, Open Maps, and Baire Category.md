# 7 Inverses, Open Maps, and Baire Category
↑ [[Functional Analysis]]

The previous chapter studied bounded linear functionals in depth. This chapter returns to bounded linear maps $M : X \to Y$ between two spaces and asks when the equation $Mx = y$ can be solved, uniquely and stably. The central result is Banach's theorem that a bounded linear bijection between Banach spaces has a bounded inverse. Its proof rests on the Baire category theorem, which says that a complete metric space cannot be a countable union of “thin” sets.
