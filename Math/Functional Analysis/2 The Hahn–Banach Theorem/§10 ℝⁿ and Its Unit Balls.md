## ℝⁿ and Its Unit Balls

Chapter 2 meets the three standard norms of $\mathbb{R}^n$ before norms are defined, as positive homogeneous subadditive functions $p_1, p_2, p_3$; their sets $\{p < 1\}$ in the plane are the disk, the square and the diamond. The level sets of $\ell(x) = a \cdot x$ are the hyperplanes and half-spaces of the separation theorem. The gauge reads the picture backwards: the gauge of a half-plane vanishes on a whole half-plane, the gauge of the unit disk is the Euclidean length, and the gauge of a square is a multiple of the maximum norm.
