# 8 Weak Convergence
↑ [[Functional Analysis]]

In finite dimensions every bounded sequence has a convergent subsequence; in infinite dimensions this fails ([[§20 Compactness and the Unit Ball#^thm-20-5|Theorem §20.5]]). This chapter weakens the notion of convergence, testing a sequence only against bounded linear functionals, to recover some of the compactness that is lost. The price is that weak limits are less well behaved: they need not respect norms or products.
