## Connections
- **What it says.** The closed unit ball of a dual space is compact in the weak-$\ast$ topology ([[§44 Weak Sequential Compactness#^def-44-3|Def. §44.3]]), for every normed space $X$. Stated in the course without proof.
- **Sequences.** Compactness in the weak-$\ast$ topology does not give convergent subsequences when $X$ is not separable ([[§44 Weak Sequential Compactness#^ex-44-2|Ex. §44.2]], [[§44 Weak Sequential Compactness#^rem-44-2|Remark in §44]]). For separable $X$ the sequential statement is [[Helly's Theorem]].
- **How (Lax).** A functional of norm at most $1$ is a point of the product of the discs $\{|c| \le \|x\|\}$, $x \in X$; the product is compact by Tychonoff's theorem, and the ball is a closed subset of it. Tychonoff's theorem is not in these notes.
- **Contrast.** In the norm topology the ball of an infinite-dimensional space is never compact: [[The Unit Ball of an Infinite-Dimensional Space Is Not Compact]].
