# 5 Inner Product Spaces
↑ [[Functional Analysis]]

A norm measures length. An inner product measures length *and* angle, and in particular gives a meaning to perpendicularity — the structure whose absence forced the quotient space to stand in for the orthogonal complement in [[§1 Linear Spaces#Quotient Spaces|§1]] and forced [[§15 Compactness and the Unit Ball#^lem-15-2|Riesz's lemma]] to stand in for an orthogonal unit vector in [[§15 Compactness and the Unit Ball|§15]]. Spaces carrying one are much closer to $\mathbb{R}^n$ than a general normed space is, and are the setting for the strongest results of the subject.
