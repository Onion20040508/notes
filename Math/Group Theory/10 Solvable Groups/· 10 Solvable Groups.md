# 10 Solvable Groups
↑ [[Group Theory]]

*Source: Worksheet 9 (Wed Oct 7).*

[[§43 Simple Groups#^def-43-1|Simple groups]] are the atoms of group theory; the next question is how they are put together. The [[§49 Solvable Groups#^def-49-1|solvable groups]] are the groups built entirely out of abelian pieces. This chapter defines them, shows that the class is closed under [[§49 Solvable Groups#^prop-49-2|subgroups]] and [[§49 Solvable Groups#^prop-49-3|quotients]], and introduces the [[§50 The Derived Series#^def-50-1|derived series]], which gives [[§50 The Derived Series#^thm-50-1|an algorithm for testing solvability]].
