# 6 Group Actions, Cosets, and Lagrange's Theorem
↑ [[Group Theory]]

*Source: Lectures (Fri Sept 11, Mon Sept 14, Wed Sept 16, Fri Sept 18); Worksheets 4 and 5; [[493 Problem Set 2|Problem Set 2]].*

The central chapter of the first half of the course. A group [[§25 Actions#^def-25-1|acting]] on a set is the same thing as a [[§25 Actions#^thm-25-3|homomorphism to a symmetric group]]; from this come [[§26 Stabilizers and Fixed Points#^def-26-1|stabilizers]], [[§27 Orbits#^def-27-1|orbits]], [[§25 Actions#^def-25-5|faithful actions]], and [[§25 Actions#^thm-25-5|Cayley's theorem]]. [[§28 Left and Right Cosets#^prop-28-1|Cosets are the orbits]] of a subgroup acting by multiplication, which yields [[§29 The Index and Lagrange's Theorem#^thm-29-2|Lagrange's theorem]]. [[§30 Orbit–Stabilizer#^thm-30-3|Orbit–stabilizer]] then ties the two threads together, with [[§30 Orbit–Stabilizer#^thm-30-6|Burnside's lemma]], the [[§30 Orbit–Stabilizer#^def-30-1|point stabilizer]] in $S_n$, and the [[§32 Linear Groups, the Cube, S₃ and A₄#^prop-32-3|rotations of the cube]] as applications.
