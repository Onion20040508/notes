# 11 The Sylow Theorems
↑ [[Group Theory]]

*Source: Worksheet 10 (handed out with Worksheet 9; not yet covered in lecture). Statements only: every proof is left to be filled in after class.*

[[§29 The Index and Lagrange's Theorem#^thm-29-2|Lagrange's theorem]] says that the [[§4 Subgroups#^def-4-6|order]] of a [[§4 Subgroups#^def-4-1|subgroup]] divides $|G|$, and [[§29 The Index and Lagrange's Theorem#^ex-29-1|its converse fails]]; Cauchy's theorem ([[§29 The Index and Lagrange's Theorem#^thm-29-6|Theorem §29.6]]) recovers it for primes. The Sylow theorems treat the largest power of a prime $p$ dividing $|G|$: [[§52 The Sylow Theorems#^thm-52-2|subgroups of that order exist]], and [[§52 The Sylow Theorems#^thm-52-4|any two are conjugate]].
