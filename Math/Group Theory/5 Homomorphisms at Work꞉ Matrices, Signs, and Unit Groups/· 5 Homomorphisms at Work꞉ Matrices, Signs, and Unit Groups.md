# 5 Homomorphisms at Work: Matrices, Signs, and Unit Groups
↑ [[Group Theory]]

*Source: Worksheet 2 and Problem Set 1 (Problems [[493 Problem Set 1#^hw-1-5|1.5]], [[493 Problem Set 1#^hw-1-6|1.6]]); lecture (Wed Sept 9).*

Three substantial applications of the homomorphism language, mostly from Problem Set 1: permutation matrices and the [[§20 Polynomial Rings, Permutation Matrices, and Representations#^prop-20-5|permutation representation]] $S_n \to GL_n(k)$; the [[§21 The Sign Homomorphism and the Alternating Group#^thm-21-3|sign homomorphism]], its kernel $A_n$, and generators for $S_n$ and $A_n$ ([[§21 The Sign Homomorphism and the Alternating Group#^thm-21-7|§21.7]], [[§21 The Sign Homomorphism and the Alternating Group#^thm-21-9|§21.9]]); and the [[§22 The Structure of Uₙ#^thm-22-2|decomposition]] of $U_n$ into prime-power pieces by the [[§9 Euclid's Lemma, Unique Factorization, and the Chinese Remainder Theorem#^thm-9-3|Chinese remainder theorem]].
