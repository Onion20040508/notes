The group $G$ of rotational symmetries of the cube $[-1,1]^3$, a subgroup of $SO_3(\mathbb{R})$ of order $24$. It is the course's main geometric example of a group action: one group acting on several different sets (faces, edges, vertices, the three pairs of opposite faces, the four body diagonals), with orbit–stabilizer giving $|G| = 24$ from each. Faithful actions embed it in $S_6$ and identify it with $S_4$, while the action on colours shows an action with a kernel. Its uses in MATH 493:

- A rotation of the cube as a permutation of the six faces ([[§25 Actions#^ex-25-3|§25]])
- Burnside's lemma: the rotations have one orbit on the faces ([[§30 Orbit–Stabilizer#^ex-30-3|§30]])
- Order $24$, and the orbits and stabilizers of faces, edges, vertices and colours ([[§32 Linear Groups, the Cube, S₃ and A₄#^prop-32-3|§32]])
- Computing the orbits and stabilizers by orbit–stabilizer ([[§32 Linear Groups, the Cube, S₃ and A₄#^pf-32-3|§32]])
- The same group acts on different sets ([[§32 Linear Groups, the Cube, S₃ and A₄#^rem-32-2|§32]])
- The action on the faces is faithful, so $G$ embeds in $S_6$ ([[§32 Linear Groups, the Cube, S₃ and A₄#^prop-32-4|§32]])
- The action on the three colours is not faithful ([[§32 Linear Groups, the Cube, S₃ and A₄#^ex-32-1|§32]])
- The action on the four body diagonals gives $G \cong S_4$ ([[§32 Linear Groups, the Cube, S₃ and A₄#^thm-32-5|§32]])
- Only faithfulness on the diagonals needs checking ([[§32 Linear Groups, the Cube, S₃ and A₄#^rem-32-3|§32]])
