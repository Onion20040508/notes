> [!question] Problem 2.1: Cosets of a Point Stabilizer in $S_n$
> Let $H = \{\sigma \in S_n : \sigma(n) = n\}$, so $H \cong S_{n-1}$.
>
> 1. Let $\alpha, \beta \in S_n$. Show that $\alpha H = \beta H$ if and only if $\alpha(n) = \beta(n)$.
> 2. Give a similar criterion for when $\alpha^{-1}(n) = \beta^{-1}(n)$.

^hw-2-1

> [!question] Problem 2.2: Isomorphisms Preserve Centers and Subgroups
> Let $\phi: G \to H$ be an isomorphism.
>
> 1. If $z$ is central in $G$, show that $\phi(z)$ is central in $H$.
> 2. If $G$ has a subgroup $G'$ other than $G$ and $\{e\}$, show that $H$ has a subgroup $H'$ other than $H$ and $\{e\}$.

^hw-2-2

*Treated in [[§16 Isomorphisms|§16]] ([[§16 Isomorphisms#^prop-16-6|Further Isomorphism Invariants]]).*

> [!question] Problem 2.3: Common Eigenvectors and Characters
> Let $k$ be a field, $V$ a $k$-vector space, and $G \leq GL(V)$. Suppose $v \neq 0$ is an eigenvector of every $g \in G$; write $gv = \lambda(g)v$.
>
> 1. Show that $\lambda: G \to k^\times$ is a group homomorphism.
> 2. Let $V$ be the space of degree-$\binom{n}{2}$ polynomials in $x_1, \ldots, x_n$, with $S_n \subseteq GL(V)$ acting by permuting variables. Describe a polynomial $f \in V$ that is an eigenvector for every $\sigma \in S_n$, and identify the corresponding $\lambda: S_n \to k^\times$.

^hw-2-3

*Treated in [[§46 Characters|§46]] ([[§46 Characters#^thm-46-2|A Common Eigenvector Yields a Character]]; (2): [[§46 Characters#^ex-46-2|Ex. §46.2]]).*

> [!question] Problem 2.4: Characters and Commutators
> Let $G$ and $A$ be groups with $A$ abelian and $\chi: G \to A$ a homomorphism (a **character**). An element $h \in G$ is a **commutator** if $h = g_1g_2g_1^{-1}g_2^{-1}$ for some $g_1, g_2 \in G$.
>
> 1. If $g_1, g_2$ are in the same conjugacy class, show $\chi(g_1) = \chi(g_2)$.
> 2. If $h$ is a commutator, show $\chi(h) = 1$.
> 3. For $n \geq 3$, show that $c = (i\,j\,k) \in S_n$ is a commutator.
> 4. Show that $s = (i\,j) \in S_n$ is not a commutator.

^hw-2-4

*Treated in [[§46 Characters|§46]] ([[§46 Characters#^prop-46-1|Characters Are Constant on Conjugacy Classes]]) and [[§47 Commutators|§47]] (Commutators: (2) [[§47 Commutators#^prop-47-1|§47.1]], (3) [[§47 Commutators#^prop-47-2|§47.2]], (4) [[§47 Commutators#^prop-47-3|§47.3]]).*
