# Problem Set 4

*Submitted. Each problem is treated in the main text, at the location indicated.*

> [!question] Problem 4.1: Products of Subgroups
> Let $A$ and $B$ be [[§4 Subgroups#^def-4-1|subgroups]] of $G$, and $AB = \{ab : a \in A,\ b \in B\}$ ([[§39 The Second and Third Isomorphism Theorems#^def-39-1|Def. §39.1]]).
>
> 1. Give an example to show that $AB$ is not necessarily a subgroup of $G$.
> 2. Suppose that $B$ is a [[§35 Normal Subgroups#^def-35-1|normal subgroup]] of $G$. Show that $AB$ is a subgroup of $G$. (The same holds if $A$ is normal; only one needs to be written up.)

^hw-4-1

*Treated in [[§39 The Second and Third Isomorphism Theorems|§39]] ($HN$ Is a Subgroup When $N$ Is Normal, [[§39 The Second and Third Isomorphism Theorems#^lem-39-1|§39.1]]; $AB$ Need Not Be a Subgroup, [[§39 The Second and Third Isomorphism Theorems#^ex-39-2|Ex. §39.2]]).*

> [!question] Problem 4.2: Normal Subgroups with Trivial Intersection
> Let $M$ and $N$ be [[§35 Normal Subgroups#^def-35-1|normal subgroups]] of $G$ with $M \cap N = \{e\}$. Show that $mn = nm$ for all $m \in M$, $n \in N$. Hint: write the conclusion as $mnm^{-1}n^{-1} = 1$.

^hw-4-2

*Treated in [[§42 Commutators|§42]] ([[§42 Commutators#^prop-42-8|§42.8]]).*

> [!question] Problem 4.3: A Normal Subgroup Inside a Finite-Index Subgroup
> Let $H$ be a subgroup of $G$ with $[G : H] = n$ ([[§27 The Index and Lagrange's Theorem#^def-27-1|Def. §27.1]]). Show that there is a [[§35 Normal Subgroups#^def-35-1|normal subgroup]] $N$ of $G$ with $N \subseteq H \subseteq G$ and $[G : N] \leq n!$. (The same problem as [[493 Problem Set 3#^hw-3-2|PS 3.2]].)

^hw-4-3

*Treated in [[§36 Sources of Normal Subgroups|§36]] ([[§36 Sources of Normal Subgroups#^thm-36-5|§36.5]]).*

> [!question] Problem 4.4: The Commutator Subgroup and the Abelianization
> Let $D(G) = \langle ghg^{-1}h^{-1} : g, h \in G \rangle$, the [[§42 Commutators#^def-42-2|commutator subgroup]].
>
> 1. Show that $D(G)$ is normal in $G$.
> 2. Let $G^{\mathrm{ab}} = G/D(G)$, the [[§42 Commutators#^def-42-3|abelianization]]. Show that $G^{\mathrm{ab}}$ is abelian.

^hw-4-4

*Treated in [[§42 Commutators|§42]] ([[§42 Commutators#^prop-42-4|§42.4]], [[§42 Commutators#^def-42-3|Def. §42.3]], [[§42 Commutators#^prop-42-6|§42.6]]).*

> [!question] Problem 4.5: Characters of the Symmetric Group
> Let $\chi: S_n \to A$ be a [[§41 Characters#^def-41-1|character]] and $a = \chi((1\,2))$. Show: (1) $\chi((i\,j)) = a$ for every transposition; (2) $a^2 = 1$; (3) $\chi(\sigma) = 1$ for $\sigma \in A_n$ and $\chi(\sigma) = a$ for $\sigma \notin A_n$. (Problems [[493 Problem Set 1#^hw-1-5|1.5]] and [[493 Problem Set 2#^hw-2-4|2.4]] may be used.)

^hw-4-5

*Treated in [[§42 Commutators|§42]] ([[§42 Commutators#^thm-42-5|§42.5]]).*
