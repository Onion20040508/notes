# 2 Arithmetic Modulo n
↑ [[Group Theory]]

*Source: Worksheet 1 (the examples $\mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z}$ and $U_n$) and class discussion (Wed Sept 2, Fri Sept 4); [[Group Theory — Problem Set 1#^hw-1-6|PS 1.6]]. Euclid's lemma, unique factorization and the Chinese remainder theorem are supplementary (cf. Pinter, MATH 412).*

The integers modulo $n$ are the first family of finite groups. This chapter builds $\mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z}$ ([[Group Theory §7 The Group ℤ∕nℤ#^def-7-1|Def. §7.1]]) from congruence and proves its operation well defined; the [[Group Theory §7 The Group ℤ∕nℤ#^lem-7-2|Descent Lemma]] used there is the template for every later well-definedness argument. It then determines which classes are invertible, giving the unit groups $U_n$ ([[Group Theory §8 Invertibility and Unit Groups#^def-8-4|Def. §8.4]]), and assembles the number theory used later: [[Group Theory §9 Euclid's Lemma, Unique Factorization, and the Chinese Remainder Theorem#^lem-9-1|Euclid's lemma]], [[Group Theory §9 Euclid's Lemma, Unique Factorization, and the Chinese Remainder Theorem#^thm-9-2|unique factorization]], and the [[Group Theory §9 Euclid's Lemma, Unique Factorization, and the Chinese Remainder Theorem#^thm-9-3|Chinese remainder theorem]].
