# 3 Permutations
↑ [[Group Theory]]

*Source: Worksheets 1–3 (the group $S_3$: [[Group Theory §13 Subgroups of S₃#^prop-13-1|WS 1.7]], [[Group Theory §14 Multiplication Tables#^ex-14-5|WS 2.4]]) and class discussion (Wed Sept 2 – Wed Sept 9); the general theory of cycles follows Pinter Ch. 7–8.*

Symmetric groups are the other basic family, and by [[Group Theory §23 Actions#^thm-23-5|Cayley's theorem]] ([[Group Theory — 6 Group Actions, Cosets, and Lagrange's Theorem|Chapter 6]]) they contain every finite group. This chapter develops cycle notation and the [[Group Theory §11 Disjoint Cycle Decomposition#^thm-11-3|disjoint cycle decomposition]], together with the two computational skills used constantly afterwards: [[Group Theory §12 Multiplying and Conjugating Cycles#^ex-12-1|multiplying cycles by tracking points]], and [[Group Theory §12 Multiplying and Conjugating Cycles#^prop-12-1|conjugating by relabelling]]. It finishes with the complete [[Group Theory §13 Subgroups of S₃#^prop-13-1|list of subgroups]] of $S_3$.
