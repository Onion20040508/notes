# 3 Permutations
↑ [[Group Theory]]

*Source: Worksheets 1–3 (the group $S_3$: [[§13 Subgroups of S₃#^prop-13-1|WS 1.7]], [[§14 Multiplication Tables#^ex-14-5|WS 2.4]]) and class discussion (Wed Sept 2 – Wed Sept 9); the general theory of cycles follows Pinter Ch. 7–8.*

Symmetric groups are the other basic family, and by [[§23 Actions#^thm-23-5|Cayley's theorem]] ([[· 6 Group Actions, Cosets, and Lagrange's Theorem|Chapter 6]]) they contain every finite group. This chapter develops cycle notation and the [[§11 Disjoint Cycle Decomposition#^thm-11-3|disjoint cycle decomposition]], together with the two computational skills used constantly afterwards: [[§12 Multiplying and Conjugating Cycles#^ex-12-1|multiplying cycles by tracking points]], and [[§12 Multiplying and Conjugating Cycles#^prop-12-1|conjugating by relabelling]]. It finishes with the complete [[§13 Subgroups of S₃#^prop-13-1|list of subgroups]] of $S_3$.
