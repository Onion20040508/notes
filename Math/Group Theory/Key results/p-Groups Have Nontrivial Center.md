## Connections
- **Proof idea.** In the [[Class Equation]], every class has size dividing pᵏ ([[§32 Conjugation as an Action and the Class Equation#^cor-32-2|§32.2]]), so the non-central ones have size divisible by p. Hence p divides |Z(G)| ([[§33 The Center#^cor-33-4|§33.4]]).
- **Prime power is needed.** S₃ (order 6) and S₄ (order 24) have trivial center ([[§32 Conjugation as an Action and the Class Equation#^ex-32-1|Ex. §32.1]]).
- **Coming later in the course.** It starts the theory of p-groups: every group of order p² is abelian (for p = 2 see [[§32 Conjugation as an Action and the Class Equation#^prop-32-6|Groups of Order 4 Are Abelian]]), every p-group is nilpotent, and Sylow theory studies the maximal p-subgroups of an arbitrary finite group.
