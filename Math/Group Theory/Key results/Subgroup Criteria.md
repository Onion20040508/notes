## Connections
- **In practice.** The one-step test (H ≠ ∅ and a, b ∈ H ⇒ ab⁻¹ ∈ H) is the shortcut recommended in the [[Group Theory Toolkit|Toolkit]]. The notes themselves mostly check e ∈ H and closure under products and inverses directly ([[§4 Subgroups#^def-4-1|Def. §4.1]]): kernels and images ([[§15 Homomorphisms#^prop-15-2|§15.2]]), stabilizers ([[§26 Stabilizers and Fixed Points#^prop-26-1|§26.1]]), the center ([[§35 The Center#^prop-35-2|§35.2]]).
- **Each condition matters.** Closure under products alone is not enough: {0, 1, 2, …} is closed under + in ℤ but has no inverses. Without nonemptiness, ∅ passes the one-step test vacuously.
- **Same idea elsewhere.** [[§3 Subspaces#^ladr-1-34|Conditions for a subspace]] (LADR 1.34), where scalar multiplication by −1 supplies inverses. The 590 version is [[§26 Algebra Prerequisites꞉ Groups#^def-26-2|590 Definition §26.2]]. As with subspaces, intersections of subgroups are subgroups ([[§4 Subgroups#^prop-4-3|§4.3]]).
