## Connections
- **In practice.** The one-step test (H ≠ ∅ and a, b ∈ H ⇒ ab⁻¹ ∈ H) is the standard way to check H ≤ G ([[Group Theory — Toolkit|Toolkit]]). Examples of subgroups checked this way: kernels and images ([[Group Theory §15 Homomorphisms#^prop-15-2|§15.2]]), stabilizers ([[Group Theory §24 Stabilizers and Fixed Points#^prop-24-1|§24.1]]), the center ([[Group Theory §33 The Center#^prop-33-2|§33.2]]).
- **Each condition matters.** Closure under products alone is not enough: {0, 1, 2, …} is closed under + in ℤ but has no inverses. Without nonemptiness, ∅ passes the one-step test vacuously.
- **Same idea elsewhere.** [[Linear Algebra 1C Subspaces#^ladr-1-34|Conditions for a subspace]] (LADR 1.34), where scalar multiplication by −1 supplies inverses. The 590 version is [[Topology §21 Algebra Prerequisites꞉ Groups#^def-21-2|590 Definition §21.2]]. As with subspaces, intersections of subgroups are subgroups ([[Group Theory §4 Subgroups#^prop-4-3|§4.3]]).
