## Connections
- **Used for.** ℚ^× ≅ ℤ^⊕∞ × ℤ/2ℤ, one coordinate per prime ([[§16 Isomorphisms#^prop-16-9|§16.9]]); Uₙ as a product over prime powers ([[§22 The Structure of Uₙ#^thm-22-2|§22.2]]) and [[§22 The Structure of Uₙ#^cor-22-3|Euler's Totient Formula]]. In a group of order pᵏ, every class size divides pᵏ and is therefore a power of p, which drives [[p-Groups Have Nontrivial Center]].
- **Proof.** Existence is by induction. Uniqueness follows from [[Euclid's Lemma]], the last link of [[§9 Euclid's Lemma, Unique Factorization, and the Chinese Remainder Theorem#^rem-9-1|The Dependency Chain in ℤ]].
- **Same idea elsewhere.** Polynomials factor uniquely into linear factors over ℂ ([[§13 Polynomials#^ladr-4-13|LADR 4.13]]), and into linear and irreducible quadratic factors over ℝ ([[§13 Polynomials#^ladr-4-16|LADR 4.16]]).
- **Also proved in [[Logic and Proofs]]:** [[§23 The Sequence of Prime Numbers#^thm-23-5|250 Thm. §23.5]] (elementary proof, uniqueness by contradiction rather than induction), with existence in [[§23 The Sequence of Prime Numbers#^prop-23-1|250 Prop. §23.1]].
