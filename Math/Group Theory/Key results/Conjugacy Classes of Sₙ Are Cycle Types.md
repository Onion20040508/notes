## Connections
- **Used for.** The class sizes of S₃ and S₄ ([[Group Theory §31 Conjugacy Classes#^ex-31-2|Ex. §31.2]]), hence their class equations ([[Group Theory §32 Conjugation as an Action and the Class Equation#^ex-32-1|Ex. §32.1]]) and their normal subgroups, e.g. {e}, V, A₄, S₄ in S₄ ([[Group Theory §36 Sources of Normal Subgroups#^ex-36-3|Ex. §36.3]]). Finding an explicit conjugating permutation: [[Group Theory §31 Conjugacy Classes#^ex-31-1|Ex. §31.1]].
- **Fails in Aₙ.** A class of Sₙ can split in Aₙ: (1 2 3) and (1 3 2) are conjugate in S₄ but not in A₄ ([[Group Theory §39 Simple Groups#^ex-39-1|Ex. §39.1]]). For 3-cycles and n ≥ 5 the class does not split ([[Group Theory §39 Simple Groups#^lem-39-8|§39.8]]), and this drives [[Aₙ Is Simple for n ≥ 5]].
- **Same idea elsewhere.** Conjugacy in GL_n(ℂ) is similarity of matrices ([[Group Theory §31 Conjugacy Classes#^def-31-3|Def. §31.3]]). There the complete invariant is the [[Jordan form]] (LADR 8.46), not a cycle type, and the characteristic polynomial suffices only for distinct eigenvalues ([[Group Theory §31 Conjugacy Classes#^prop-31-3|§31.3]], [[Group Theory §31 Conjugacy Classes#^rem-31-1|What the Argument Uses]]).
