## Connections
- **Used for.** The class sizes of S₃ and S₄ ([[§33 Conjugacy Classes#^ex-33-2|Ex. §33.2]]), hence their class equations ([[§34 Conjugation as an Action and the Class Equation#^ex-34-1|Ex. §34.1]]) and their normal subgroups, e.g. {e}, V, A₄, S₄ in S₄ ([[§39 Sources of Normal Subgroups#^ex-39-3|Ex. §39.3]]). Finding an explicit conjugating permutation: [[§33 Conjugacy Classes#^ex-33-1|Ex. §33.1]].
- **Fails in Aₙ.** A class of Sₙ can split in Aₙ: (1 2 3) and (1 3 2) are conjugate in S₄ but not in A₄ ([[§43 Simple Groups#^ex-43-1|Ex. §43.1]]). For 3-cycles and n ≥ 5 the class does not split ([[§43 Simple Groups#^lem-43-8|§43.8]]), and this drives [[Aₙ Is Simple for n ≥ 5]].
- **Same idea elsewhere.** Conjugacy in GL_n(ℂ) is similarity of matrices ([[§33 Conjugacy Classes#^def-33-3|Def. §33.3]]). There the complete invariant is the [[Jordan form]] (LADR 8.46), not a cycle type, and the characteristic polynomial suffices only for distinct eigenvalues ([[§33 Conjugacy Classes#^prop-33-4|§33.4]], [[§33 Conjugacy Classes#^rem-33-1|What the Argument Uses]]).
