## Connections
- **Used for.** [[Subgroups of ℤ]] (every subgroup is nℤ), hence [[Bézout's Identity]] and the rest of [[Group Theory §9 Euclid's Lemma, Unique Factorization, and the Chinese Remainder Theorem#^rem-9-1|The Dependency Chain in ℤ]]. Also congruence classes ([[Group Theory §6 Divisibility and Congruence#^prop-6-2|§6.2]]), [[Subgroups of Cyclic Groups Are Cyclic]], the [[Classification of Cyclic Groups]], and the fact that the order of φ(g) divides the order of g ([[Group Theory §16 Isomorphisms#^prop-16-4|§16.4]]).
- **The pattern.** "Take the smallest positive element, then divide with remainder" ([[Group Theory §9 Euclid's Lemma, Unique Factorization, and the Chinese Remainder Theorem#^rem-9-2|The Division-Algorithm Pattern]]). The remainder lies in the subgroup and is smaller, so it must be 0.
- **Same idea elsewhere.** [[Linear Algebra 4 Polynomials#^ladr-4-9|Division algorithm for polynomials]] (LADR 4.9) uses degree in place of size. The same least-element argument shows that the polynomials annihilating T are the multiples of the minimal polynomial ([[Linear Algebra 5B The Minimal Polynomial#^ladr-5-29|LADR 5.29]]), the analogue of subgroups of ℤ being nℤ.
