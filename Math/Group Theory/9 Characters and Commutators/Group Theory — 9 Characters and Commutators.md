# 9 Characters and Commutators
↑ [[Group Theory]]

*Source: Lecture (Wed Sept 23); PS 2.3 and 2.4.*

[[Group Theory §40 Characters#^def-40-1|Characters]] are homomorphisms to abelian groups. They [[Group Theory §40 Characters#^prop-40-1|cannot distinguish conjugate elements]] and they [[Group Theory §41 Commutators#^prop-41-1|kill every commutator]], which makes them a practical tool for proving that an element is *not* a commutator; the chapter ends by determining [[Group Theory §41 Commutators#^thm-41-5|the commutator subgroup]] of $S_n$. Characters are also the first step toward the representation theory of the second half of the course.
