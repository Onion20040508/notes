# 9 Characters and Commutators
↑ [[Group Theory]]

*Source: Lecture (Wed Sept 23); PS 2.3 and 2.4.*

[[§41 Characters#^def-41-1|Characters]] are homomorphisms to abelian groups. They [[§41 Characters#^prop-41-1|cannot distinguish conjugate elements]] and they [[§42 Commutators#^prop-42-1|kill every commutator]], which makes them a practical tool for proving that an element is *not* a commutator; the chapter determines [[§42 Commutators#^thm-42-5|the commutator subgroup]] of $S_n$, and with it [[§42 Commutators#^cor-42-7|all characters]] of $S_n$, and ends with the [[§42 Commutators#^def-42-3|abelianization]] and [[§42 Commutators#^prop-42-8|commuting normal subgroups]] (Problem Set 4). Characters are also the first step toward the representation theory of the second half of the course.
