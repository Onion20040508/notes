*The pair $(72, 30)$ is the textbook's first example of a greatest common divisor. Its Chapter 4 appearances are gathered here in course order; the items themselves stay in their sections.*

[[§11 Properties of Finite Sets#^ex-11-4|Example §11.4]] found $\gcd(72, 30) = 6$ by listing divisors.
