## Connections
- **Used for.** With the standard charts, ℂPⁿ becomes a compact smooth manifold of dimension 2n, indeed a complex manifold ([[§8 Differentiable Structures#^thm-8-7|§8.7]], [[§8 Differentiable Structures#^cor-8-8|§8.8]]), and ℂP¹ is homeomorphic to S² ([[§8 Differentiable Structures#^prop-8-9|§8.9]]). The same argument gives ℝPⁿ ([[§8 Differentiable Structures#^cor-8-10|§8.10]]), and S²ⁿ⁺¹ → ℂPⁿ is the Hopf fibration ([[§16 Fibrations#^ex-16-1|Ex. §16.1]]).
- **The pattern, and where it breaks.** The relation is open because S¹ acts by homeomorphisms, and the graph is closed by compactness ([[§3 New Spaces from Old꞉ Subspaces, Products, and Quotients#^rem-3-19|Where This Is Going]]). The origin must be removed: if ℂ^× scales all of ℂⁿ⁺¹, every neighbourhood of the orbit {0} is the whole quotient ([[§3 New Spaces from Old꞉ Subspaces, Products, and Quotients#^ex-3-8|Ex. §3.8]]). The action is by isometries, uniformly over the group, which is what fails for the hyperbola action of ℝ⁺ ([[§3 New Spaces from Old꞉ Subspaces, Products, and Quotients#^rem-3-17|§3, Remark]], [[§6 Group Actions and Orbit Spaces#^ex-6-6|Ex. §6.6]]).
- **Same idea elsewhere.** It is the case of U(1) acting on S²ⁿ⁺¹ ([[§6 Group Actions and Orbit Spaces#^ex-6-3|Ex. §6.3]]) of [[Orbit Spaces of Compact Groups Are Hausdorff]]. The real analogue is 590's projective space Pⁿ = Sⁿ/(x ∼ −x) ([[§28 Fundamental Group of Some Surfaces#^def-28-2|590 Def. §28.2]], [[Projective plane]]), where S² → P² is a 2-sheeted covering map ([[§28 Fundamental Group of Some Surfaces#^thm-28-1|590 §28.1]]).
- **Coming later in the course.** In the Lie group part of the course, ℂPⁿ is the homogeneous space U(n + 1)/(U(1) × U(n)), which so far has only been checked by counting dimensions.
