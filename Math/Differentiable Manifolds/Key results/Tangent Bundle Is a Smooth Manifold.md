## Connections
- **Used for.** TM is a manifold of dimension 2m fibring over M with fibre ℝᵐ, with the zero section a submanifold ([[§35 The Tangent Bundle#^cor-35-3|§35.3]]); vector fields are its sections ([[§35 The Tangent Bundle#^def-35-4|Def. §35.4]]). The same charts with dual bases make T*M a vector bundle ([[§35 The Tangent Bundle#^prop-35-5|§35.5]]).
- **How the charts transform.** Over an overlap the base point moves by the transition map of M and the vector by its Jacobian, the change of coordinates of [[§28 The Differential in Coordinates#^cor-28-4|§28.4]]; covector components move by the inverse transpose. These are the change-of-basis formula and the transpose matrix of a dual map ([[§10 Invertibility and Isomorphisms#^ladr-3-84|LADR 3.84]], [[§12 Duality#^ladr-3-132|LADR 3.132]]).
- **Locally a product.** The trivializations identify each TU with U × ℝᵐ, but a fibration need not be a global product, as the Möbius band shows ([[§34 Fibrations#^ex-34-2|Ex. §34.2]]). Unlike the Hopf fibration, which has no section ([[§38 Projective Spaces and the Hopf Fibration#^rem-38-1|Sections Need Not Exist]]), TM always has the zero section.
- **Coming later in the course.** Vector fields and flows: a vector field is a section of TM, and its integral curves fit together into a flow on M.
