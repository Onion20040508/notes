## Connections
- **Used for.** The components of a manifold are open, coincide with its path components, and are manifolds of the same dimension ([[§2 Topological Manifolds#^cor-2-10|§2.10]]). Openness of the components is what lets second countability bound their number ([[§2 Topological Manifolds#^prop-2-6|§2.6]], [[§2 Topological Manifolds#^rem-2-10|Remark]]).
- **Only local Euclideanness is used.** Each point has a path-connected neighbourhood, and the set of points reachable from a base point is then open and closed ([[§2 Topological Manifolds#^pf-2-9|proof]]). For general spaces the statement fails: the topologist's sine curve is connected but not path connected ([[§14 Connected Subspaces of ℝ#^ex-14-7|590 Ex. §14.7]]).
- **Same idea elsewhere.** The converse, path connected ⇒ connected, holds in every space ([[§14 Connected Subspaces of ℝ#^thm-14-4|590 §14.4]]), and the clopen argument is [[§13 Connected Spaces#^lem-13-1|590 §13.1]]. Since π₁ of a path-connected space does not depend on the base point up to isomorphism ([[Basepoint Independence of π₁]]), neither does π₁ of a connected manifold.
