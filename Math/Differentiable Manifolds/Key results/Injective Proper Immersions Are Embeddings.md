## Connections
- **Used for.** On a compact manifold every injective immersion is an embedding ([[§37 Embeddings#^cor-37-6|§37.6]]), and then its image is a submanifold by [[Images of Embeddings Are Submanifolds]].
- **How.** Proper maps into manifolds are closed ([[§37 Embeddings#^prop-37-4|§37.4]]), by an argument that uses that manifolds are locally compact ([[§2 Topological Manifolds#^prop-2-12|§2.12]]). So F is a closed continuous bijection onto F(M), hence a homeomorphism onto its image.
- **Properness is needed.** The hook is an injective immersion that is not proper, since a compact disc around P has non-compact preimage, and it is not an embedding ([[§36 Immersions#^ex-36-2|Ex. §36.2]]). The irrational line on the torus is another such example ([[§37 Embeddings#^prop-37-11|Prop. §37.11]]).
- **Same idea elsewhere.** The point-set prototype is [[Bijection from Compact to Hausdorff is a Homeomorphism]] (590 §15.7). Properness is also the hypothesis of Ehresmann's theorem ([[§34 Fibrations#^thm-34-3|§34.3]]).
