## Connections
- **Used for.** [[ℂPⁿ Is Hausdorff and Second Countable]], and G/H for second countable G ([[§7 Homogeneous Spaces#^cor-7-10|§7.10]]), hence the Grassmannians ([[§7 Homogeneous Spaces#^cor-7-13|§7.13]]). Orbit relations of continuous actions are open ([[§6 Group Actions and Orbit Spaces#^lem-6-3|§6.3]]), so every orbit space of a second countable space is second countable.
- **Openness is needed.** Collapsing ℤ ⊂ ℝ to one point gives a quotient that is not even first countable there. The relation is not open: the saturation of (−1/2, 1/2) is (−1/2, 1/2) ∪ ℤ ([[§3 New Spaces from Old꞉ Subspaces, Products, and Quotients#^ex-3-2|Ex. §3.2]], [[§3 New Spaces from Old꞉ Subspaces, Products, and Quotients#^rem-3-9|Why the Diagonal]]).
- **Same idea elsewhere.** An open surjection carries a basis to a basis ([[§3 New Spaces from Old꞉ Subspaces, Products, and Quotients#^lem-3-23|§3.23]]) and is a quotient map ([[§12 Quotient Topology#^prop-12-2|590 §12.2]]). In 590 second countability passes to subspaces and countable products ([[§18 Countability Axioms#^thm-18-3|590 §18.3]]); quotients need the extra hypothesis.
- **Coming later in the course.** Second countability is what makes partitions of unity available, and they are how integrals of differential forms over a whole manifold are defined.
