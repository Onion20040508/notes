## Connections
- **Used for.** [[ℂPⁿ Is Hausdorff and Second Countable]], and G/H for second countable G ([[§12 The Topology of G∕H and Real Grassmannians#^cor-12-5|§12.5]]), hence the Grassmannians ([[§12 The Topology of G∕H and Real Grassmannians#^cor-12-8|§12.8]]). Orbit relations of continuous actions are open ([[§10 Group Actions and Orbit Spaces#^lem-10-3|§10.3]]), so every orbit space of a second countable space is second countable.
- **Openness is needed.** Collapsing ℤ ⊂ ℝ to one point gives a quotient that is not even first countable there. The relation is not open: the saturation of (−1/2, 1/2) is (−1/2, 1/2) ∪ ℤ ([[§4 Quotient Spaces and Open Maps#^ex-4-4|Ex. §4.4]], [[§4 Quotient Spaces and Open Maps#^rem-4-4|Why the Diagonal]]).
- **Same idea elsewhere.** An open surjection carries a basis to a basis ([[§5 Quotient Maps#^lem-5-6|§5.6]]) and is a quotient map ([[§12 Quotient Topology#^prop-12-2|590 §12.2]]). In 590 second countability passes to subspaces and countable products ([[§18 Countability Axioms#^thm-18-3|590 §18.3]]); quotients need the extra hypothesis.
- **Coming later in the course.** Second countability is what makes partitions of unity available, and they are how integrals of differential forms over a whole manifold are defined.
