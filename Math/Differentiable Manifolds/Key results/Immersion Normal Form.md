## Connections
- **Used for.** Locally, the image of an immersion is a submanifold of codimension n − m ([[§18 Immersions#^cor-18-2|§18.2]]).
- **Only local.** Globally the image need not be a submanifold. γ(t) = (t² − 1, t³ − t) crosses itself, making an X at the origin ([[§18 Immersions#^ex-18-1|Ex. §18.1]]). An injective immersion can run back into a point of its own image, making a T there, and then it is not a homeomorphism onto its image ([[§18 Immersions#^ex-18-2|Ex. §18.2]]).
- **Same idea elsewhere.** It mirrors the [[Submersion Normal Form]]: there F is locally a projection, here an inclusion. The case m = n is the [[Local Diffeomorphism Criterion]], from the [[Inverse Function Theorem (several variables)]]. In ℝⁿ⁺ᵏ the internal description of a manifold is a parametrization with injective derivative that is a homeomorphism onto its image ([[§9 Manifolds in Euclidean Space#^def-9-1|Def. §9.1]]), and [[§9 Manifolds in Euclidean Space#^thm-9-1|§9.1]] matches it with the level-set description.
- **Coming later in the course.** Embeddings, the immersions that are homeomorphisms onto their images, have submanifolds as images, and Whitney's theorem embeds every manifold in some ℝᴺ.
