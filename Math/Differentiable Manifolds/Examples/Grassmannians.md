The Grassmannian $\mathrm{Gr}_k(\mathbb{R}^n)$ of $k$-dimensional linear subspaces of $\mathbb{R}^n$, with $\mathrm{Gr}_1(\mathbb{R}^n) = \mathbb{RP}^{n-1}$. It is the course's showcase for giving a bare set a topology through a transitive group action: $\mathrm{O}(n)$ acts transitively with isotropy $\mathrm{O}(k) \times \mathrm{O}(n-k)$, which identifies $\mathrm{Gr}_k(\mathbb{R}^n)$ with a compact Hausdorff coset space of expected dimension $k(n-k)$. For $k = 1$ it recovers the projective spaces of [[Real and complex projective spaces]], and the group side is in [[Classical groups O(n), U(n), SL(n,ℝ)]]. Its uses in MATH 591:

- Definition, and $\mathrm{Gr}_1(\mathbb{R}^n) = \mathbb{RP}^{n-1}$ ([[§15 The Topology of G∕H and Real Grassmannians#^def-15-3|§15]])
- On the index: lines in $\mathbb{R}^n$ form $\mathbb{RP}^{n-1}$ ([[§15 The Topology of G∕H and Real Grassmannians#^rem-15-3|§15]])
- $\mathrm{O}(n)$ acts transitively on $\mathrm{Gr}_k(\mathbb{R}^n)$ ([[§15 The Topology of G∕H and Real Grassmannians#^prop-15-6|§15]])
- The isotropy of the standard $k$-plane is $\mathrm{O}(k) \times \mathrm{O}(n-k)$ ([[§15 The Topology of G∕H and Real Grassmannians#^prop-15-7|§15]])
- $\mathrm{Gr}_k(\mathbb{R}^n) \cong \mathrm{O}(n)/(\mathrm{O}(k) \times \mathrm{O}(n-k))$ is compact, Hausdorff and second countable ([[§15 The Topology of G∕H and Real Grassmannians#^cor-15-8|§15]])
- Expected dimension $k(n-k)$, and the missing charts ([[§15 The Topology of G∕H and Real Grassmannians#^rem-15-4|§15]])
- For $k = 1$ the Grassmannian topology agrees with the quotient topology on $\mathbb{RP}^n$ ([[§18 Projective Spaces as Smooth Manifolds#^prop-18-6|§18]])
- Dimension check: $\dim \mathrm{O}(n) - \dim \mathrm{O}(k) - \dim \mathrm{O}(n-k) = k(n-k)$ ([[§25 The Geometric Tangent Space#^rem-25-9|§25]])
