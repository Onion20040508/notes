The Grassmannian $\mathrm{Gr}_k(\mathbb{R}^n)$ of $k$-dimensional linear subspaces of $\mathbb{R}^n$, with $\mathrm{Gr}_1(\mathbb{R}^n) = \mathbb{RP}^{n-1}$. It is the course's showcase for giving a bare set a topology through a transitive group action: $\mathrm{O}(n)$ acts transitively with isotropy $\mathrm{O}(k) \times \mathrm{O}(n-k)$, which identifies $\mathrm{Gr}_k(\mathbb{R}^n)$ with a compact Hausdorff coset space of expected dimension $k(n-k)$. For $k = 1$ it recovers the projective spaces of [[Real and complex projective spaces]], and the group side is in [[Classical groups O(n), U(n), SL(n,ℝ)]]. Its uses in MATH 591:

- Definition, and $\mathrm{Gr}_1(\mathbb{R}^n) = \mathbb{RP}^{n-1}$ ([[§12 The Topology of G∕H and Real Grassmannians#^def-12-3|§12]])
- On the index: lines in $\mathbb{R}^n$ form $\mathbb{RP}^{n-1}$ ([[§12 The Topology of G∕H and Real Grassmannians#^rem-12-3|§12]])
- $\mathrm{O}(n)$ acts transitively on $\mathrm{Gr}_k(\mathbb{R}^n)$ ([[§12 The Topology of G∕H and Real Grassmannians#^prop-12-6|§12]])
- The isotropy of the standard $k$-plane is $\mathrm{O}(k) \times \mathrm{O}(n-k)$ ([[§12 The Topology of G∕H and Real Grassmannians#^prop-12-7|§12]])
- $\mathrm{Gr}_k(\mathbb{R}^n) \cong \mathrm{O}(n)/(\mathrm{O}(k) \times \mathrm{O}(n-k))$ is compact, Hausdorff and second countable ([[§12 The Topology of G∕H and Real Grassmannians#^cor-12-8|§12]])
- Expected dimension $k(n-k)$, and the missing charts ([[§12 The Topology of G∕H and Real Grassmannians#^rem-12-4|§12]])
- For $k = 1$ the Grassmannian topology agrees with the quotient topology on $\mathbb{RP}^n$ ([[§14 Projective Spaces as Smooth Manifolds#^prop-14-6|§14]])
- Dimension check: $\dim \mathrm{O}(n) - \dim \mathrm{O}(k) - \dim \mathrm{O}(n-k) = k(n-k)$ ([[§20 Tangent Spaces I꞉ The Geometric Picture#^rem-20-9|§20]])
