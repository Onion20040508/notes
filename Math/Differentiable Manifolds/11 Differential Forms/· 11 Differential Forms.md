# 11 Differential Forms
↑ [[Differentiable Manifolds]]

*Lecture 28. This chapter, planned to run until Thanksgiving, is differential forms and Stokes' theorem; it follows Lee, Chapters 14–16. It will need manifolds with boundary, orientations, integrals of forms, and the general Stokes theorem $\int_{\partial S} \alpha = \int_S d\alpha$. It opens with the theorem to be generalized, recalled from Calculus III in this course's notation ([[§73 Stokes' Theorem in Calculus III|§73]]); [[§74 The Integrand Is a Two-Form|§74]] reads its surface integrand as a two-form, and [[§75 Multilinear Algebra|§75]] starts the multilinear algebra of $k$-forms.*
