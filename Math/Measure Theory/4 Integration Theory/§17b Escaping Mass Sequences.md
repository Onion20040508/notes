Sequences that converge to $0$ pointwise while their mass does not go away: it slides off to infinity or piles up on a shrinking interval. In Chapter 4 each one breaks a limit theorem of integration by violating its finiteness or domination hypothesis. All appearances are listed in [[Escaping mass sequences]].

## Escaping Mass Sequences

The decreasing MCT needs a finite integral, Fatou's inequality can be strict, and the DCT needs an integrable dominator; the figure after [[§15a The Dominated Convergence Theorem#^rem-15-3|the comparison of convergence theorems]] shows both sequences.
