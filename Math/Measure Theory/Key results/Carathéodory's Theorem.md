## Connections
- **Generalizes.** It is the abstract form of [[Lebesgue Measurable Sets Form a σ-Algebra]] (§10.3). The ℝⁿ proof goes through [[§11 Lebesgue Measurable Sets#^thm-11-1|closure properties]] (§11.1) and [[§11 Lebesgue Measurable Sets#^lem-11-2|finite additivity on test sets]] (§11.2), and it uses only the three axioms of an [[§12 Borel Sets and Measure Spaces#^def-12-3|outer measure]] (Def. §12.3), which [[Properties of Lebesgue Outer Measure]] verifies for m* ([[§12 Borel Sets and Measure Spaces#^rem-12-1|Rem. §11.1]]).
- **Status in the notes.** It is stated without its own proof. Rem. §11.1 points back to the §11 argument, which is where the proof lives.
- **Output.** Restricting μ* to the μ*-measurable sets gives a measure ([[§12 Borel Sets and Measure Spaces#^prop-12-3|Prop. §12.3]]) and so a [[§12 Borel Sets and Measure Spaces#^def-12-7|measure space]] (Def. §12.7). For Lebesgue outer measure this is (ℝⁿ, ℳ, m).
- **Technique.** The criterion of [[§12 Borel Sets and Measure Spaces#^def-12-4|Def. §12.4]] is what [[Measure Theory Problem-Solving Techniques#^rem-19-8|Technique 4: The Carathéodory Splitting Trick]] proves or exploits.
